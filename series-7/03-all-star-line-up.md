# 第 3 集 Ep 3：All-star Line-up｜全明星阵容

**剧情简介 Synopsis:** 五的家族要开全明星演唱会，可一半演员被困住了！五号到二十五号想办法，凑出三十到五十救场。/ It's showtime for the Family of Five, but half the performers are stranded! Five to Twenty-five must figure out how to make Thirty to Fifty.

**角色 Characters:** 五号 Five、十五 Fifteen、二十五 Twenty-five、三十 Thirty、五十 Fifty

**剧本 Script:**

*（场景：舞台后台，演员们着急 / Scene: backstage, performers in a panic）*

**Five（五号）:** Oh no! Half our stars are stranded! The show must go on! / 糟糕！一半明星被困住了！演出必须继续！

**Fifteen（十五）:** Five, ten, fifteen… we're only up to twenty-five! / 五、十、十五……我们只凑到二十五！

**Twenty-five（二十五）:** We need thirty! Five more! / 我们需要三十！再来五个！

*（五号加入，"25, 30!" / Five joins in: "25, 30!"）*

**Thirty（三十）:** I'm Thirty! Six fives! The show is saved! / 我是三十！六个五！演出有救啦！

**Fifty（五十）:** And I'm Fifty! Ten fives! Let's hear it for the Family of Five! / 我是五十！十个五！为五的家族欢呼吧！

**All（大家）:** 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 35, 40, 45, 50! / 全明星阵容登场！
