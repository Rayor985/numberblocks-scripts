# 第 1 集 Ep 1：Grid Unlocked｜解锁网格

**剧情简介 Synopsis:** 乘法表们的 5×5 能量网格被锁住了！Numberblocks 们翻转身体、拼出长方形，合力解锁网格。/ The Times Tables' 5 by 5 booster grid is locked! The Numberblocks flip and turn themselves into rectangles to unlock it.

**角色 Characters:** 五号 Five、五乘法表 Five Times Table、二十五 Twenty-five

**剧本 Script:**

*（场景：能量网格前，网格一片灰暗 / Scene: in front of the booster grid, all grey and locked）*

**Five Times Table（五乘法表）:** Oh no! Our 5 by 5 booster grid is locked! / 糟糕！我们的 5×5 能量网格被锁住了！

**Five（五号）:** Don't worry! Five fives make twenty-five! / 别担心！五个五就是二十五！

*（五个五号叠在一起，"5, 10, 15, 20, 25！" / Five Fives stack up: "5, 10, 15, 20, 25!"）*

**Twenty-five（二十五）:** I'm Twenty-five! A super square, 5 by 5! / 我是二十五！超级正方形，5×5！

**Five Times Table（五乘法表）:** Turn yourself around… now fit the grid! / 把身体转一转……对准网格放进去！

*（二十五旋转身体，嵌入网格，网格亮起 / Twenty-five turns around, fits into the grid, and it lights up）*

**All（大家）:** Grid unlocked! Hooray! / 网格解锁！万岁！
