# 第 14 集 Ep 14：Nine's Time to Shine｜九的高光时刻

**剧情简介 Synopsis:** 九乘法表唱起歌，揭秘九的倍数的规律：加十再减一，从九一路数到九十！/ Meet the Nine Times Table as he sings the nines trick: add ten and take one away, from Nine to Ninety!

**角色 Characters:** 九乘法表 Nine Times Table

**剧本 Script:**

*（场景：聚光灯下，九乘法表登场 / Scene: under the spotlight, the Nine Times Table appears）*

**Nine Times Table（九乘法表）:** It's my time to shine! Here's the nines trick! / 到我的高光时刻了！看九的秘诀！

🎵 Add ten and take one away, / 🎵 加十再减一，

🎵 That's how the nines come out to play! / 🎵 九的倍数就出现！

🎵 9, 18, 27, 36, / 🎵 9、18、27、36，

🎵 45, 54, 63, 72, 81, 90 — we're done! / 🎵 45、54、63、72、81、90——完成！

**Nine Times Table（九乘法表）:** See? 9 becomes 18, 18 becomes 27… just add ten, take one away! / 看到了吗？9 变成 18，18 变成 27……加十减一就行！
