# 第 12 集 Ep 12：Seventy-two's Super Surprise｜七十二的超级惊喜

**剧情简介 Synopsis:** Octoblock 被捣蛋鬼 Octonaughty 困住了！他向七十二求救——超级大长方形前来救场！/ Octoblock is trapped by Octonaughty! He calls on Seventy-two for a super surprise rescue!

**角色 Characters:** Octoblock（八号）Octoblock、捣蛋鬼 Octonaughty、七十二 Seventy-two

**剧本 Script:**

*（场景：Octoblock 被网困住 / Scene: Octoblock caught in a net）*

**Octoblock（Octoblock）:** Help! Octonaughty trapped me! I need a super-duper rectangle! / 救命！Octonaughty 把我困住了！我需要一个超级大长方形！

**Octonaughty（捣蛋鬼）:** Hehehe! No one can save you now! / 嘿嘿嘿！现在没人能救你了！

**Seventy-two（七十二）:** Seventy-two to the rescue! I'm 8 by 9 — AND 9 by 8! / 七十二前来救援！我是 8×9——也是 9×8！

*（七十二翻转身体挣脱陷阱，救出 Octoblock / Seventy-two flips around, breaks the trap, and frees Octoblock）*

**Octoblock（Octoblock）:** You turned yourself around! Amazing! / 你把自己翻转了！太厉害了！

**Seventy-two（七十二）:** That's the super surprise — rectangles can turn! / 这就是超级惊喜——长方形会翻转！
