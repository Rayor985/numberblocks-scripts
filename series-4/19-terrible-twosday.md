# 第 19 集 Ep 19：Terrible Twosday｜糟糕的星期二

**剧情简介 Synopsis:** 星期二分享日：7÷2=3……还多出1个！有余数一点也不可怕！ / Sharing Tuesday: 7 ÷ 2 = 3… with 1 left over! Remainders aren't scary at all!

**角色 Characters:** 七号 Seven、二号 Two、三号 Three、十号 Ten

**剧本 Script:**

*（星期二，分享日 / Tuesday, sharing day）*

**Seven（七号）:** Oh no! It's Twosday! Everything must be shared into twos! / 糟糕！今天是星期二！所有东西都要按两份来分！

*（七号分成两组：3和3，还剩1个 / Seven splits into two groups: 3 and 3, with 1 left over）*

**Seven（七号）:** 7 ÷ 2 = 3… with 1 left over! / 7÷2=3……还剩1个！

**Two（二号）:** A remainder! Terrible! / 有余数！太糟糕了！

**Ten（十号）:** Let's try me! 10 ÷ 3 = ? / 试试我！10÷3=？

*（十号分成三组：3、3、3，还剩1个 / Ten splits into three groups: 3, 3, 3, with 1 left over）*

**Three（三号）:** 10 ÷ 3 = 3 remainder 1! / 10÷3=3余1！

**Seven（七号）:** Remainders aren't terrible — they're terrific! / 余数一点也不可怕——它超有趣！

**All（大家）:** Hooray for remainders! / 为余数欢呼！
