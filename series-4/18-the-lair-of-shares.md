# 第 18 集 Ep 18：The Lair of Shares｜分享洞穴

**剧情简介 Synopsis:** 分享洞穴里藏着除法的秘密：公平分享就是除法，8÷2=4！ / The Lair of Shares hides the secret of division: sharing fairly is division! 8 ÷ 2 = 4!

**角色 Characters:** 八号 Eight、四号 Four、九号 Nine、三号 Three

**剧本 Script:**

*（洞穴里，宝箱上写着"分享" / In the cave, a treasure chest reads "SHARE"）*

**Eight（八号）:** The Lair of Shares! Let's share my blocks fairly! / 分享洞穴！我们来公平分我的方块！

*（八号分成两组 / Eight splits into two groups）*

**Four（四号）:** Eight shared into two groups… four and four! / 八分成两组……四和四！

**Four（四号）:** Two fours! Fair and square! / 两个四！公平又漂亮！

**Eight（八号）:** 8 ÷ 2 = 4! That's division! / 8÷2=4！这就是除法！

**Nine（九号）:** My turn! Nine shared into three groups! / 轮到我了！九分成三组！

*（九号分成三个三号 / Nine splits into three Threes）*

**Three（三号）:** 9 ÷ 3 = 3! Three, three and three! / 9÷3=3！三、三、三！

**Nine（九号）:** Three threes! Everyone gets the same! / 三个三！人人一样多！

**All（大家）:** Sharing fairly is dividing! / 公平分享就是除法！
