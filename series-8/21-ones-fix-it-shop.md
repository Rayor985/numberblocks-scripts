# 第 21 集 Ep 21：One's Fix-It Shop｜一号修理铺

**剧情简介 Synopsis:** 一号修理铺把两块碎片拼回一个整体，缺多少就补多少。 / One's Fix-It Shop puts two pieces back into one whole, adding exactly what is missing.

**角色 Characters:** 一号 One、二号 Two、三号 Three、四号 Four、机器人 Robo

**剧本 Script:**

*（修理铺的桌上摆着裂成两半的玩具小车 / A toy car split in half sits on the repair-shop bench）*

**Robo（机器人）:** Beep! My toy car is broken into two parts! / 哔！我的玩具小车裂成了两部分！

**One（一号）:** Don't worry, Robo! One's Fix-It Shop is open! Let's take a look... / 别担心，机器人！一号修理铺开张啦！让我看看……

**One（一号）:** One part is three blocks, the other part is two blocks! / 一部分是三个方块，另一部分是两个方块！

**Three（三号）:** Three and two make a whole five! / 三加二等于完整的五！

*（两部分咔哒一声拼回去，小车完好如初 / The two parts click together — the car is whole again）*

**Two（二号）:** But what if a piece is missing? / 可如果缺了一块呢？

**Three（三号）:** Then find the number that completes the whole! / 那就找出能补全整体的那个数！

**Four（四号）:** This wheel needs ten blocks, but we only have six! / 这个轮子要十个方块，可我们只有六个！

**One（一号）:** Six needs four more to make ten! Four blocks, coming up! / 六再加四就是十！四个方块，马上就来！

**Robo（机器人）:** Beep-beep! Four and six make ten! My wheel is round again! / 哔哔！四加六等于十！我的轮子又圆啦！

**Three（三号）:** 🎵 Fix it up, make it whole! / 🎵 修一修，变完整！

**Three（三号）:** 🎵 Every part plays its role! / 🎵 每个部分都重要！

*（修理铺门口挂起"全部修好"的牌子 / An "All Fixed!" sign hangs outside the shop）*

**All（大家）:** Parts plus parts: one whole again! / 部分加部分：又是一个完整的整体！
