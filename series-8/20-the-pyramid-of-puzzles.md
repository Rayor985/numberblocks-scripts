# 第 20 集 Ep 20：The Pyramid of Puzzles｜谜题金字塔

**剧情简介 Synopsis:** 谜题金字塔按条形图的高度从低到高排列，只有把图形对上才能登顶。 / The puzzle pyramid rises in bar-graph order, and only matching the right heights lets everyone climb it.

**角色 Characters:** 一号 One、二号 Two、三号 Three、四号 Four、五号 Five

**剧本 Script:**

*（沙漠里立着五级台阶的谜题金字塔，每级高矮不一 / A five-step puzzle pyramid rises in the desert, each step a different height）*

**Four（四号）:** Look! Each step is a bar: one, two, three, four, five — going up in order! / 看！每一级都是一条条形：一、二、三、四、五——越往上越高！

**Two（二号）:** I take a low step — two! Pairs start small! / 我走矮台阶——二！成双成对从小开始！

**Three（三号）:** Then me — three! I'm one step taller, watch my show! / 再到我——三！我比它高一级，看我的表演！

*（三号轻盈地跳上第三级台阶 / Three hops lightly onto the third step）*

**One（一号）:** Bouncy steps for bouncy climbers! Boing! / 台阶弹弹的，爬的人也弹弹的！蹦！

**Four（四号）:** My turn — four! Four square steps, steady and strong! / 轮到我——四！四级方方正正的台阶，稳稳当当！

**Five（五号）:** And the tallest step is mine — five! Give me a high five! / 最高的那级是我的——五！来击个掌！

**Three（三号）:** Five high fives for five high steps! / 五级高台阶，五个击掌！

**One（一号）:** Wait — what about the very first step? It's the tiniest one! / 等等——最底下那级呢？那是最小的一级！

**One（一号）:** One! The smallest bar starts it all! / 一！最小的条形是起点！

*（五个人按从矮到高的顺序站成一排，塔顶的宝箱缓缓打开 / The five line up from shortest to tallest, and the chest at the top swings open）*

**Five（五号）:** 🎵 Climb the bars from low to high! / 🎵 从矮到高往上爬！

**Five（五号）:** 🎵 Reach the top and touch the sky! / 🎵 登上塔顶摸到天！

**Two（二号）:** Low bars, high bars, all in order — the pyramid reads like a graph! / 矮条形、高条形，排得整整齐齐——金字塔就是一张统计图！

**All（大家）:** Puzzle pyramid climbed — treasure time! / 谜题金字塔登顶——开宝箱啦！
