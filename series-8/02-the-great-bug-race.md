# 第 2 集 Ep 2：The Great Bug Race｜大虫赛跑

**剧情简介 Synopsis:** 小虫们沿着不同形状的跑道赛跑，谁跑的路线最长？这要看周长。 / The bugs race around differently shaped tracks. Whose route is longest? It all comes down to perimeter.

**角色 Characters:** 一号 One、二号 Two、三号 Three、四号 Four、十六 Sixteen、二十 Twenty、点点 Dotty、图图 Tutu、小方虫 Little Square Bug

**剧本 Script:**

*（草地上画着三条形状不同的跑道 / Three tracks of different shapes are drawn on the grass）*

**Twenty（二十）:** Welcome to the Great Bug Race! Run all the way around your track! / 欢迎来到大虫赛跑！沿着自己的跑道跑一整圈！

**Little Square Bug（小方虫）:** My square has four equal sides, so my route is short and neat. / 我的跑道是正方形，四条边一样长，路线又短又整齐。

**Tutu（图图）:** My rectangle is long, so I have farther to go. / 我的跑道是长方形，比较长，所以我跑得更远。

**Four（四号）:** Perimeter is the distance all the way around a shape. / 周长就是绕着图形走一整圈的长度。

**Sixteen（十六）:** Add up every side and you have the perimeter! / 把每一条边加起来，就是周长！

*（虫儿们冲过终点，观众欢呼 / The bugs cross the finish line and the crowd cheers）*

**Dotty（点点）:** My route was longer, but I still had fun! / 我的路线更长，可我一样开心！

**All（大家）:** Round and round: that's perimeter! / 绕一圈，再绕一圈：这就是周长！
