# 第 2 集 Ep 2：The Great Bug Race｜大虫赛跑

**剧情简介 Synopsis:** 小虫们沿着不同形状的跑道赛跑，谁跑的路线最长？这要看周长。 / The bugs race around differently shaped tracks. Whose route is longest? It all comes down to perimeter.

**角色 Characters:** 一号 One、二号 Two、三号 Three、四号 Four、十六 Sixteen、二十 Twenty、点点 Dotty、图图 Tutu、小方虫 Little Square Bug

**剧本 Script:**

*（草地上画着三条形状不同的跑道 / Three tracks of different shapes are drawn on the grass）*

**Twenty（二十）:** Welcome to the Great Bug Race! Run all the way around your track! / 欢迎来到大虫赛跑！绕着自己的跑道跑一整圈！

**One（一号）:** On your marks... get set... GO! / 各就各位……预备……跑！

**Little Square Bug（小方虫）:** My track is a square! Four sides, all the same! / 我的跑道是正方形！四条边都一样长！

**Tutu（图图）:** Mine is a rectangle — long sides and short sides! / 我的是长方形——有长边也有短边！

**Dotty（点点）:** Mine is a triangle! Three sides to run around! / 我的是三角形！要绕着三条边跑！

**Four（四号）:** Perimeter is the distance all the way around a shape! / 周长就是绕着图形走一整圈的长度！

**Two（二号）:** Let's count the sides in pairs! Two and two! / 我们两两数边！二加二！

**Sixteen（十六）:** Add up every side — that's the perimeter! / 把每条边加起来，就是周长！

*（虫儿们冲过终点，观众欢呼 / The bugs dash across the finish line and the crowd cheers）*

**Little Square Bug（小方虫）:** Four plus four plus four plus four — sixteen steps! / 四加四加四加四——十六步！

**Tutu（图图）:** My long track took more steps, but what a race! / 我的长跑道用了更多步，跑得真过瘾！

**Dotty（点点）:** Longer or shorter, we all made it to the end! / 不管长短，我们都跑到了终点！

**Twenty（二十）:** And the winner is... everyone! / 获胜者是……所有人！

**All（大家）:** 🎵 Round and round and round we go! / 🎵 绕一圈，绕一圈，一直绕下去！

**All（大家）:** 🎵 Add the sides — that's perimeter, you know! / 🎵 把边加起来——这就是周长！
