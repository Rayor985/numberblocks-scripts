# 第 18 集 Ep 18：Five's Fruit Festival｜五号水果节

**剧情简介 Synopsis:** 水果节用象形图摆出最受欢迎的水果，一眼就能比出多少。 / The fruit festival uses a pictogram, so everyone can see which fruit is most popular at a glance.

**角色 Characters:** 一号 One、二号 Two、五号 Five、六号 Six、七号 Seven、八号 Eight、九号 Nine

**剧本 Script:**

*（广场上挂起水果节的彩旗，摊位上摆满水果图片 / Festival flags hang across the square, and fruit pictures fill the stalls）*

**Five（五号）:** Welcome to the fruit festival! Every fruit picture stands for one vote! / 欢迎来到水果节！每张水果图片代表一票！

**One（一号）:** I vote apple! One juicy red apple for me! / 我投苹果！一个多汁的大红苹果归我！

**Two（二号）:** Apples have four pictures — that's four votes, two pairs! / 苹果有四张图片——四票，两对！

**Seven（七号）:** Oranges have six pictures — six votes! Lucky six! / 橙子有六张图片——六票！幸运的六！

**Eight（八号）:** Pears have only two... poor little pears. / 梨只有两张……可怜的小梨。

**Two（二号）:** Two pears, two votes — at least they make a pair! / 两个梨，两票——至少它们成双成对！

**Six（六号）:** Let's line the pictures up in columns and compare! / 把图片排成一列列来比一比！

*（苹果、橙子、梨的图片排成三列，橙子那列最高 / Apple, orange and pear pictures line up in three columns — the orange column stands tallest）*

**Nine（九号）:** The tallest column is the easiest to see — oranges win! / 最高的一列最显眼——橙子赢了！

**Seven（七号）:** Six votes for oranges — more than four and two together! / 橙子六票——比四加二还多！

**Five（五号）:** 🎵 Count the pictures, line them high! / 🎵 数图片，排高高！

**Five（五号）:** 🎵 The tallest column wins the prize! / 🎵 最高的一列拿第一！

*（装满橙子的果篮被高高抬上领奖台 / The basket of oranges is lifted high onto the winner's step）*

**One（一号）:** One clear picture tells us everything — pictograms are magic! / 一张清楚的图告诉我们一切——象形图真神奇！

**All（大家）:** Sample, count, compare: happy fruit festival! / 取样、计数、比较：水果节快乐！
