The Pyramid of Puzzles

谜题金字塔
📖 剧情简介

谜题金字塔按条形图的高度从低到高排列，只有把图形对上才能登顶。

The puzzle pyramid rises in bar-graph order, and only matching the right heights lets everyone climb it.
