# 第 17 集 Ep 17：Back in the Box!｜回到盒子里！

**剧情简介 Synopsis:** 散落的积木要靠正字记号清点，一笔一笔记下来，数量就不会错。 / Loose blocks are counted with tally marks: one stroke at a time, and nothing goes missing.

**角色 Characters:** 一号 One、二号 Two、三号 Three、四号 Four、五号 Five

**剧本 Script:**

*（积木撒了一地，盒子空空地张着嘴 / Blocks spill all over the floor while the empty box sits with its mouth wide open）*

**Five（五号）:** Oh no, what a muddle! Let's count them back into the box! / 糟糕，乱成一团！我们把它们数回盒子里！

**One（一号）:** I'll tally as we go! One, two, three, four... / 我来边数边记！一、二、三、四……

**One（一号）:** And a line across for five! That's one neat group! / 第五笔横着一划！整齐的一组！

**Two（二号）:** A group of five is easy to spot — two groups, that's pairs! / 五个一组真好认——两组，那就是成双成对！

**Three（三号）:** Three groups of five... that's ten, and five more is fifteen! / 三组五个……那是十，再加五个是十五！

*（积木一块接一块跳进盒子 / Blocks hop into the box one by one）*

**Four（四号）:** Four groups make twenty — four square groups, all tidy! / 四组就是二十——四个方方正正的组，真整齐！

**Three（三号）:** Twenty blocks in four neat groups — I could juggle them all! / 二十块积木分四组——我能把它们全抛起来！

**Five（五号）:** Wait — two loose ones are hiding under the chair! / 等等——椅子下面还藏着两个散的！

**One（一号）:** Twenty-one, twenty-two — tally them too! / 二十一、二十二——也记下来！

**Two（二号）:** Two loose ones make a pair — pairs never get lost! / 两个散的凑成一对——成双成对不会丢！

**Five（五号）:** 🎵 Tally one, tally all! / 🎵 记一个，记全部！

**Five（五号）:** 🎵 Count them in, none shall fall! / 🎵 数进盒里，一个不落！

*（最后一块积木蹦进盒子，盖子啪地合上 / The last block bounces in and the lid snaps shut）*

**All（大家）:** Tally, check, and every block is back in the box! / 记一记、查一查，积木全都回盒啦！
