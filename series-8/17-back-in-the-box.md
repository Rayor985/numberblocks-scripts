# 第 17 集 Ep 17：Back in the Box!｜回到盒子里！

**剧情简介 Synopsis:** 散落的积木要靠正字记号清点，一笔一笔记下来，数量就不会错。 / Loose blocks are counted with tally marks: one stroke at a time, and nothing goes missing.

**角色 Characters:** 一号 One、二号 Two、三号 Three、四号 Four、五号 Five

**剧本 Script:**

*（积木撒了一地，盒子空空的 / Blocks spill across the floor and the box sits empty）*

**Five（五号）:** Count them into the box with tally marks. / 用正字记号把它们数进盒子里。

**One（一号）:** One, two, three, four—now a line across for five! / 一、二、三、四——第五笔横过去！

**Two（二号）:** A group of five is easy to spot. / 五个一组，一眼就能看出。

**Three（三号）:** Another group of five: that's ten blocks. / 又一组五个：一共十个。

**Four（四号）:** Two groups plus two loose ones make twelve. / 两组加两个散的，一共十二个。

*（最后一块跳进盒子 / The last block hops into the box）*

**Five（五号）:** Tally, check, and every block is back! / 记一记、查一查，积木全回家了！
