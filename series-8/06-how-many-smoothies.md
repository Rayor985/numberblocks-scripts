# 第 6 集 Ep 6：How Many Smoothies?｜多少杯果昔

**剧情简介 Synopsis:** 五号和朋友调换果昔的排列顺序，发现总数一点也没变。 / Five and friends swap the order of their smoothies and discover the total never changes.

**角色 Characters:** 一号 One、二号 Two、三号 Three、四号 Four、五号 Five、数字小球 Numberblobs

**剧本 Script:**

*（果汁吧里，两排果昔排得整整齐齐 / At the juice bar, two rows of smoothies stand in order）*

**Five（五号）:** Two smoothies here, three there. How many altogether? / 这边两杯，那边三杯，一共多少杯？

**Two（二号）:** Two plus three is five! / 二加三等于五！

**Three（三号）:** Now swap the rows: three plus two! / 现在把两排调换：三加二！

**Five（五号）:** Three and two still make five! / 三加二还是五！

**Four（四号）:** The order changes, but the total stays the same. / 顺序变了，总数却不变。

*（Numberblobs 排成一个大大的 5 / The Numberblobs form a giant 5）*

**One（一号）:** That's the commutative property—switch and it's still equal! / 这就是加法交换律——交换以后依然相等！

**All（大家）:** How many smoothies? Five happy faces! / 多少杯果昔？五张开心的笑脸！
