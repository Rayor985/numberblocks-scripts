# 第 6 集 Ep 6：How Many Smoothies?｜多少杯果昔

**剧情简介 Synopsis:** 五号和朋友调换果昔的排列顺序，发现总数一点也没变。 / Five and friends swap the order of their smoothies and discover the total never changes.

**角色 Characters:** 一号 One、二号 Two、三号 Three、四号 Four、五号 Five、数字小球 Numberblobs

**剧本 Script:**

*（果汁吧里，两排果昔排得整整齐齐 / At the juice bar, two rows of smoothies stand in order）*

**Five（五号）:** Smoothie time! Two here, three there! / 果昔时间到！这边两杯，那边三杯！

**Two（二号）:** Two plus three is five! / 二加三等于五！

**Three（三号）:** Now let's swap! Three plus two! / 现在调换一下！三加二！

**Five（五号）:** Three and two still make five! / 三加二还是等于五！

**One（一号）:** One sip for me... yummy! / 我先喝一口……真好喝！

**Four（四号）:** The order changed, but the total stays the same! / 顺序变了，总数却不变！

**Two（二号）:** Two rows, two ways — both make five! / 两排，两种摆法——都是五！

**Five（五号）:** High five! Five smoothies, five friends! / 击掌！五杯果昔，五个朋友！

*（数字小球排成一个大大的 5 / The Numberblobs line up to form a giant 5）*

**One（一号）:** Look! The Numberblobs made a giant Five! / 看！数字小球拼成了一个大大的五！

**Three（三号）:** Switch it around, it's still the same — what a magic rule! / 调换顺序，结果不变——多神奇的规律！

**All（大家）:** 🎵 Two plus three, three plus two! / 🎵 二加三，三加二！

**All（大家）:** 🎵 Swap them round — still five for you! / 🎵 换来换去——还是五！
