# 第 10 集 Ep 10：The Best House Band in Numberland｜数字乐园最佳乐队

**剧情简介 Synopsis:** 乐队要按部分与整体排出最好的阵容，六到十的每个数都能拆成好听的组合。 / The house band arranges its line-up by part and whole, finding musical ways to split every number from six to ten.

**角色 Characters:** 一号 One、二号 Two、三号 Three、四号 Four、五号 Five、六号 Six、七号 Seven、八号 Eight、九号 Nine、十号 Ten、数字小球 Numberblobs

**剧本 Script:**

*（舞台灯光亮起，乐器已经就位 / Stage lights come up and the instruments are ready）*

**Six（六号）:** Welcome to the show! Every number has parts that make a whole! / 欢迎来看演出！每个数都能拆成组成它的部分！

**Five（五号）:** Seven is five and two—guitar and drums! / 七是五加二——吉他加鼓！

**Seven（七号）:** Five fingers and two more—hear my guitar roar! / 五根手指再加二——听我的吉他吼！

**Eight（八号）:** Eight is six and two, or five and three—watch me juggle the beat! / 八是六加二，也可以是五加三——看我玩转节拍！

**Nine（九号）:** Nine is six and three—all-knowing me! / 九是六加三——全知的我！

**Ten（十号）:** Ten is five and five—double high five! / 十是五加五——双倍击掌！

**Numberblobs（数字小球）:** 🎵 Part and part make a whole—give us a call! / 🎵 部分加部分组成整体——叫上我们！

**All（大家）:** 🎵 Six and four, seven and three, every number's melody! / 🎵 六和四，七和三，每个数都有旋律！

**All（大家）:** 🎵 Five and five make ten, let's play it all again! / 🎵 五加五等于十，再来一遍！

*（全场跟着节拍拍手 / The whole audience claps along）*

**Three（三号）:** Three and three make six—hear my trumpet tricks! / 三加三等于六——听我的小号绝技！

**Four（四号）:** Four and two make six—square and steady on the bass! / 四加二等于六——贝斯稳稳方方正正！

**All（大家）:** Parts make a whole, and the whole band plays on! / 部分组成整体，整支乐队继续演奏！
