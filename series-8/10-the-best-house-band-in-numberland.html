The Best House Band in Numberland

数字乐园最佳乐队
📖 剧情简介

乐队要按部分与整体排出最好的阵容，六到十的每个数都能拆成好听的组合。

The house band arranges its line-up by part and whole, finding musical ways to split every number from six to ten.
