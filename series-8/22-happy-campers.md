# 第 22 集 Ep 22：Happy Campers｜快乐露营队

**剧情简介 Synopsis:** 露营队把食物平均分给每个人，分得一样多，大家就都开心。 / The campers share food equally; when every share is the same, everyone is happy.

**角色 Characters:** 一号 One、二号 Two、三号 Three、四号 Four

**剧本 Script:**

*（傍晚，营火在空地上噼啪作响 / Evening: a campfire crackles in the clearing）*

**One（一号）:** Camping time! I've got one big backpack! / 露营时间到！我背了一个大背包！

**Two（二号）:** And I've got two torches—one for each hand! / 我带了两支火把——左右手各一支！

**Three（三号）:** Look at my three-legged stool! Perfect for sitting! / 看我的三脚凳！坐着正合适！

**Four（四号）:** And I've got a square tent with four corners! / 我带了方形帐篷，四个角！

*（营火边摆着一篮子棉花糖 / A basket of marshmallows waits by the campfire）*

**Four（四号）:** Eight marshmallows for four campers—everyone gets two! / 八个棉花糖分给四个露营者——每人两个！

**One（一号）:** Two each! Count with me: one, two! / 每人两个！跟我数：一、二！

**Two（二号）:** Twelve berries, three friends: four each! / 十二颗莓果，三个朋友——每人四颗！

**Three（三号）:** Four for you, four for you, four for me—equal shares! / 你四颗，你四颗，我四颗——一样多！

**One（一号）:** Equal shares mean fair shares! / 一样多就是公平！

**Four（四号）:** 🎵 Share it out, share it fair, / 🎵 分一分，分得公平，

**All（大家）:** 🎵 Equal pieces for all to share! / 🎵 每人一份一样多！

*（火光映着一张张笑脸 / Firelight flickers over smiling faces）*

**Two（二号）:** Nobody gets more, nobody gets less! / 谁也不多，谁也不少！

**Three（三号）:** That's what makes us happy campers! / 这就是快乐露营队的秘诀！

**Four（四号）:** One share each, one happy camp! / 每人一份，露营快乐！
