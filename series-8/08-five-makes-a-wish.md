# 第 8 集 Ep 8：Five Makes a Wish｜五号许愿

**剧情简介 Synopsis:** 五号对着流星许愿，每次都要凑满五颗星，加法一次比一次顺。 / Five wishes on a shooting star and fills up to five each time, making addition smoother every go.

**角色 Characters:** 一号 One、二号 Two、三号 Three、四号 Four、五号 Five、六号 Six、七号 Seven、八号 Eight、九号 Nine、十号 Ten

**剧本 Script:**

*（夜空中划过一颗流星 / A shooting star streaks across the night sky）*

**Five（五号）:** Look! A shooting star! Quick, everyone—make a wish! / 看！流星！快，大家一起许愿！

**Five（五号）:** I need five stars to make my wish come true. Who can help me? / 一个愿望要五颗星，谁能帮我？

**Two（二号）:** I bring two shiny stars! / 我带来两颗亮闪闪的星！

**Three（三号）:** And I bring three! Count with me: two and three make five! / 我带来三颗！跟我数：二加三等于五！

**Five（五号）:** My wish is ready! Give me a high five! / 愿望凑齐啦！来击个掌！

**Four（四号）:** Wait—four and one make five too! / 等等——四加一也等于五！

**One（一号）:** One little star from me! / 我的一颗小星星！

**Five（五号）:** Every way to make five ends the same: five! / 凑成五的方法各不相同，结果都是五！

**Five（五号）:** 🎵 Five little stars, shining bright! / 🎵 五颗小星星，闪闪亮！

**All（大家）:** 🎵 Two and three, four and one, wish upon five and have some fun! / 🎵 二加三，四加一，对五许愿真有趣！

*（五颗星连成一只闪亮的手 / The five stars join into a shining hand）*

**Six（六号）:** Six wants a wish too—five and one make six! / 六也想要愿望——五加一等于六！

**Five（五号）:** Then we need six stars! Let's add one more! / 那我们要六颗星！再加一颗！

**All（大家）:** Add them up and the wish comes through! / 加起来，愿望就实现！
