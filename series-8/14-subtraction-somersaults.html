Subtraction Somersaults

减法翻筋斗
📖 剧情简介

减法也能越过十：先减到十，再把剩下的减掉，翻个筋斗答案就出来了。

Subtraction can bridge through ten too—take away down to ten, then take the rest, and the answer tumbles out.
