How to Count

怎么数数
📖 剧情简介

野餐时少了一块煎饼！是煎饼小偷干的。数一数：一个一个点，最后一个数就是总数！

A flapjack is missing from the picnic! The Flapjack Snaffler took it. To count: touch each one, say one number at a time — the last number tells how many!
