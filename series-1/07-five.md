# 第 7 集 Ep 7：Five｜五号之歌

**剧情简介 Synopsis:** 五号登场！她最爱击掌：一、二、三、四、五！/ Five arrives! She loves high fives: one, two, three, four, five!

**角色 Characters:** 五号 Five

**剧本 Script:**

*（数字乐园，五号挥手出场 / Numberland, Five waves hello）*

**Five（五号）:** Hello! I'm Five! / 你好！我是五号！

**Five（五号）:** I have five blocks! One, two, three, four, five! / 我有五个方块！一、二、三、四、五！

**Five（五号）:** Give me a high five! / 来击个掌！

*（五号举起手 / Five holds up her hand）*

**Five（五号）:** Five fingers on my hand! / 我手上有五根手指！

**Five（五号）:** 🎵 Five, five, high-five Five! / 🎵 五，五，击掌的五！

**Five（五号）:** 🎵 Count to five and feel alive! / 🎵 数到五，精神爽！

**Five（五号）:** Five is more than four! / 五比四大！

**Five（五号）:** One, two, three, four, five — all my friends in a line! / 一、二、三、四、五——朋友们排成行！

**Five（五号）:** High five, everybody! / 大家来击掌！
