# 第 6 集 Ep 6：Four｜四号之歌

**剧情简介 Synopsis:** 四号登场！他是方方正正的正方形：四条边、四个角！/ Four arrives! He's a square through and through: four sides, four corners!

**角色 Characters:** 四号 Four

**剧本 Script:**

*（数字乐园，四号迈着方步出场 / Numberland, Four marches in squarely）*

**Four（四号）:** Hello! I'm Four! / 你好！我是四号！

**Four（四号）:** I have four blocks! One, two, three, four! / 我有四个方块！一、二、三、四！

**Four（四号）:** Look at me — I'm a square! / 看我——我是正方形！

**Four（四号）:** Four sides, four corners! / 四条边，四个角！

*（四号摆成正方形 / Four arranges into a square）*

**Four（四号）:** Two by two makes a square! / 二乘二就是正方形！

**Four（四号）:** 🎵 Four, four, square-tastic Four! / 🎵 四，四，正方形之四！

**Four（四号）:** 🎵 Count my sides: one, two, three, four! / 🎵 数我的边：一、二、三、四！

**Four（四号）:** Four wheels on a car go round and round! / 汽车有四个轮子转圈圈！

**Four（四号）:** Four legs on a table, steady and strong! / 桌子有四条腿，又稳又壮！

**Four（四号）:** Square power! / 正方形力量！
