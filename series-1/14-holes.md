# 第 14 集 Ep 14：Holes｜洞洞

**剧情简介 Synopsis:** 数字乐园出现了好多洞！掉进去减一，爬出来加一！/ Holes appear in Numberland! Fall in, take away one; climb out, add one!

**角色 Characters:** 二号 Two、三号 Three、四号 Four

**剧本 Script:**

*（数字乐园，地上多了几个洞 / Numberland, holes in the ground）*

**Three（三号）:** Oh no! Holes everywhere! / 糟糕！到处都是洞！

*（三号掉进洞里 / Three falls into a hole）*

**Three（三号）:** Help! I'm stuck down here! / 救命！我卡在下面了！

**Four（四号）:** I'll pull you out! Three and one more is four — that's me! / 我拉你出来！三加一等于四——就是我！

*（四号把三号拉出来 / Four pulls Three out）*

**Three（三号）:** Thank you! Watch out for holes! / 谢谢！小心脚下的洞！

*（二号也掉进去了 / Two falls in too）*

**Two（二号）:** Oops! Down I go! / 哎哟！我掉下来了！

**Three（三号）:** My turn to help! Two and one more is three! / 轮到我帮忙！二加一等于三！

*（三号拉出二号 / Three pulls Two out）*

**Two（二号）:** Phew! Add one, take away one — holes are tricky! / 呼！加一减一——洞洞真调皮！

**All（大家）:** In you go, out you come — plus one, minus one! / 掉进去减一，爬出来加一！
