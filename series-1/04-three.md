# 第 4 集 Ep 4：Three｜三号之歌

**剧情简介 Synopsis:** 三号登场！她是杂耍高手：三个球，一、二、三！/ Three arrives! She's a juggling star: three balls — one, two, three!

**角色 Characters:** 三号 Three

**剧本 Script:**

*（数字乐园，三号抛着球出场 / Numberland, Three arrives juggling）*

**Three（三号）:** Hello! I'm Three! / 你好！我是三号！

**Three（三号）:** I have three blocks! One, two, three! / 我有三个方块！一、二、三！

**Three（三号）:** Watch me juggle! One ball, two balls, three balls! / 看我杂耍！一个球、两个球、三个球！

*（三号抛起三个球 / Three juggles three balls）*

**Three（三号）:** Three is more than two! / 三比二多！

**Three（三号）:** 🎵 Three, three, jolly Three! / 🎵 三，三，快乐的三！

**Three（三号）:** 🎵 Juggling high for you and me! / 🎵 为你为我抛得高！

**Three（三号）:** Three cheers! Hip, hip, hooray! / 欢呼三声！嘿、嘿、万岁！

**Three（三号）:** One, two, three — catch! / 一、二、三——接住！

**Three（三号）:** Three's a crowd, and I love crowds! / 三人成群，我爱热闹！
