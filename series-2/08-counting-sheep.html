Counting Sheep

数绵羊
📖 剧情简介

六号想哄调皮的小羊睡觉，却发现：两个头（或三个头）比一个头强——把六分成相等的几份！

Six tries to get the cheeky sheep to sleep and discovers that two (or three) heads are better than one — sharing six into equal parts!
