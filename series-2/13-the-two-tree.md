# 第 13 集 Ep 13：The Two Tree｜双数树

**剧情简介 Synopsis:** Numberblocks 们发现了一棵神奇的"双数树"：每次摇一摇，就减掉二！/ The Numberblocks find a magic Two Tree — every shake takes away two!

**角色 Characters:** 十号 Ten、八号 Eight、树 Tree

**剧本 Script:**

*（场景：神奇的双数树下 / Scene: under the magic Two Tree）*

**Ten（十号）:** Wow! A tree that grows… twos? / 哇！一棵长着……二的树？

*（摇树，掉下两个方块 / Shake the tree, two blocks fall off）*

**Eight（八号）:** Ten take away two leaves… Eight! / 十减二等于……八！

**Ten（十号）:** Shake again! Eight take away two is Six! / 再摇一次！八减二等于六！

**Six（六号）:** Six take away two is Four! Two take away two is… / 六减二等于四！二减二等于……

**All（大家）:** …Zero! 10, 8, 6, 4, 2, 0! / ……零！10、8、6、4、2、0！
