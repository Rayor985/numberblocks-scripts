# 第 3 集 Ep 3：Eight｜八号

**剧情简介 Synopsis:** 数字乐园迎来超级英雄八号——朋友们都叫他 Octoblock！/ Numberland is rocked by the arrival of superblock Eight, known to his friends as Octoblock!

**角色 Characters:** 八号 Eight（Octoblock）、七号 Seven

**剧本 Script:**

*（场景：八号从天而降 / Scene: Eight swoops down from the sky）*

**Eight（八号）:** Octoblock is here! Who needs help? / Octoblock 来啦！谁需要帮助？

**Seven（七号）:** Wow! Eight blocks! You're so… octagonal! / 哇！八个方块！你真是……八角形的！

**Eight（八号）:** I can split into four twos, or two fours! / 我可以分成四个二，或者两个四！

*（八号摆出各种形状 / Eight arranges into different shapes）*

**Seven（七号）:** Amazing! You're a real superhero! / 太厉害了！你真是超级英雄！

**Eight（八号）:** Octoblock — always ready to save the day! / Octoblock——随时准备拯救世界！
