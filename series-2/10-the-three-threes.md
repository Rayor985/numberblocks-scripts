# 第 10 集 Ep 10：The Three Threes｜三个三

**剧情简介 Synopsis:** 九号需要帮手，摇身一变成马戏团明星"三个三"！把九分成相等的三份！/ When Nine needs a helping hand, he becomes the Three Threes — sharing nine into three equal parts!

**角色 Characters:** 九号 Nine、三号 Three（×3）

**剧本 Script:**

*（场景：马戏团 / Scene: the circus）*

**Nine（九号）:** I need a helping hand… I know! Split into three! / 我需要帮手……有了！分成三份！

*（九号分成三个三号 / Nine splits into three Threes）*

**Three Threes（三个三）:** We're the Three Threes! Three, three and three! / 我们是"三个三"！三、三、三！

**Nine（九号）:** Three threes make nine! Now let's juggle! / 三个三等于九！开始杂耍吧！

*（三个三号一起抛球 / The Three Threes juggle together）*

**All（大家）:** The Three Threes — the greatest circus act! / "三个三"——最棒的马戏表演！
