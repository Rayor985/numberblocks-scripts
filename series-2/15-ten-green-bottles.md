# 第 15 集 Ep 15：Ten Green Bottles｜十个绿瓶子

**剧情简介 Synopsis:** 墙上有十个绿瓶子，一个不小心掉了下来……从十数到一！/ Ten green bottles sitting on the wall — and if one should accidentally fall… count all the way down from ten to one!

**角色 Characters:** 十号 Ten、瓶子们 Bottles

**剧本 Script:**

*（场景：墙上排着十个绿瓶子 / Scene: ten green bottles on the wall）*

**All（大家）:** 🎵 Ten green bottles sitting on the wall, / 🎵 十个绿瓶子排排站，

🎵 Ten green bottles sitting on the wall, / 🎵 十个绿瓶子排排站，

🎵 And if one green bottle should accidentally fall, / 🎵 要是一个绿瓶子不小心掉下来，

*（一个瓶子掉下来 / One bottle falls）*

**Nine（九号）:** There'll be nine green bottles sitting on the wall! / 就剩下九个绿瓶子排排站！

**All（大家）:** 🎵 Nine green bottles… eight green bottles… / 🎵 九个绿瓶子……八个绿瓶子……

*（瓶子一个个掉落：7, 6, 5, 4, 3, 2, 1 / The bottles fall one by one: 7, 6, 5, 4, 3, 2, 1）*

**All（大家）:** …and no green bottles sitting on the wall! / ……墙上一个绿瓶子也不剩啦！
