# 第 8 集 Ep 8：Counting Sheep｜数绵羊

**剧情简介 Synopsis:** 六号想哄调皮的小羊睡觉，却发现：两个头（或三个头）比一个头强——把六分成相等的几份！/ Six tries to get the cheeky sheep to sleep and discovers that two (or three) heads are better than one — sharing six into equal parts!

**角色 Characters:** 六号 Six、小羊 Sheep

**剧本 Script:**

*（场景：夜晚，羊圈 / Scene: night time, the sheep pen）*

**Six（六号）:** Time for bed, little sheep! One… two… three… / 小羊们，该睡觉了！一……二……三……

*（小羊们调皮地跳来跳去 / The sheep bounce around cheekily）*

**Sheep（小羊）:** Baa! We don't want to sleep! / 咩！我们不想睡！

**Six（六号）:** Hmm… two heads are better than one! Split into two threes! / 嗯……两个头比一个头强！分成两个三！

*（六号分成两个三号，一人哄一半小羊 / Six splits into two Threes, each soothing half the sheep）*

**Six（六号）:** Three and three make six… and now the sheep are asleep! / 三加三等于六……小羊们睡着啦！
