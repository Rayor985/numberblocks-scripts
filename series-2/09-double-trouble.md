# Numberblocks 第二季第 9 集：Double Trouble｜翻倍麻烦（完整剧本）

> 说明：BBC 未公开发布官方逐字剧本，以下为根据官方剧情主线、以剧集风格创作的中英对照完整剧本（原创台词）。
>
> **剧情简介 Synopsis:** 一号发现了一张藏宝图，独自勇闯"翻倍地牢"，寻找传说中的金苹果。每穿过一扇翻倍门，她就翻一倍：1→2→4→8，直达 Octoblock！可出来的时候，又该怎么办呢？
> One finds a treasure map and explores the Double Dungeon of Doom in search of the golden apples, doubling all the way up to Octoblock!
>
> **数学目标 Maths:** 翻倍与减半（1、2、4、8）；把 8 分成相等的几份 / Doubling and halving (1, 2, 4, 8); partitioning 8 into equal groups
>
> **角色 Characters:** 一号 One、二号 Two、四号 Four、八号 Eight（Octoblock）

---

## 第一场：藏宝图 / Scene 1: The Treasure Map

*（数字乐园，阳光明媚。一号蹦蹦跳跳，踢到一个卷轴 / Numberland, sunny day. One skips along and kicks a scroll）*

**One（一号）:** Ouch! What's this? / 哎哟！这是什么？

*（展开卷轴，眼睛发亮 / She unrolls the scroll, eyes shining）*

**One（一号）:** Wow! A treasure map! / 哇！一张藏宝图！

**One（一号）:** It says… "The Double Dungeon of Doom… golden apples inside!" / 上面写着……"翻倍地牢……里面有金苹果！"

**One（一号）:** Golden apples? Yummy! I'm going on an adventure! / 金苹果？好吃！我要去探险！

*（一号扛起小背包，出发 / One grabs a little backpack and sets off）*

---

## 第二场：地牢入口 / Scene 2: The Dungeon Entrance

*（阴森的石门，牌子上写着 "DOUBLE DUNGEON OF DOOM"。第一扇翻倍门闪着光 / Spooky stone doors with a sign: "DOUBLE DUNGEON OF DOOM". The first doubling gate glows）*

**One（一号）:** Ooh… spooky! But I'm brave! / 哇……好吓人！但我很勇敢！

**One（一号）:** This gate says… "Step through to DOUBLE!" / 这扇门上写着……"穿过去就会翻倍！"

**One（一号）:** Double? What's double? Only one way to find out! / 翻倍？什么是翻倍？试试就知道！

*（一号穿过门，闪光，"1… 2！" / One steps through. Flash! "1… 2!"）*

**Two（二号）:** I'm Two! One doubled is two! / 我是二号！一翻倍就是二！

**Two（二号）:** One and one make two! Double trouble begins! / 一加一等于二！翻倍麻烦开始啦！

---

## 第三场：地牢深处 / Scene 3: Deeper In

*（第二扇更大的翻倍门 / A second, bigger doubling gate）*

**Two（二号）:** Another doubling gate! Here I go! / 又一扇翻倍门！我来啦！

*（二号穿过门，闪光，"2… 4！" / Two steps through. Flash! "2… 4!"）*

**Four（四号）:** I'm Four! Two doubled is four! / 我是四号！二翻倍就是四！

**Four（四号）:** Two and two make four! I'm getting bigger! / 二加二等于四！我越来越大啦！

---

## 第四场：宝藏室 / Scene 4: The Treasure Chamber

*（最后一扇巨大的翻倍门，门后金光闪闪 / One last giant doubling gate, golden light behind it）*

**Four（四号）:** The golden apples! Just one more gate! / 金苹果！只剩最后一扇门了！

*（四号穿过门，大闪光，"4… 8！" / Four steps through. Big flash! "4… 8!"）*

**Eight（八号）:** I'm Eight! Octoblock! Four doubled is eight! / 我是八号！Octoblock！四翻倍就是八！

*（宝藏室里堆满金苹果 / The treasure chamber is piled high with golden apples）*

**Eight（八号）:** The golden apples! We did it! / 金苹果！我们成功啦！

**All（大家）:** 🎵 Double one is two, double two is four, / 🎵 一翻倍是二，二翻倍是四，

🎵 Double four is eight — let's double some more! / 🎵 四翻倍是八——继续翻倍吧！

---

## 第五场：怎么出去？/ Scene 5: How Do We Get Out?

*（八号走向出口，小门 / Eight heads for the exit — a tiny door）*

**Eight（八号）:** Hmm… this door is too small for Eight! How do we get out? / 嗯……这扇门对八号来说太小了！我们怎么出去？

*（门上写着："HALVE to go back!" / The door reads: "HALVE to go back!"）*

**Eight（八号）:** Halve? That means split into two equal halves! / 减半？就是分成相等的两半！

*（八号分成两个四号 / Eight splits into two Fours）*

**Eight（八号）:** Eight halved is… four and four! / 八减半就是……两个四！

*（一个四号穿过减半门，闪光，"4… 2！" / One Four steps through the halving gate. Flash! "4… 2!"）*

**Two（二号）:** Four halved is… two! / 四减半就是……二！

*（二号穿过减半门，闪光，"2… 1！" / Two steps through. Flash! "2… 1!"）*

**One（一号）:** Two halved is… one! I'm One again! / 二减半就是……一！我又变回一号啦！

---

## 第六场：分享金苹果 / Scene 6: Sharing the Golden Apples

*（回到数字乐园，一号抱着一篮金苹果 / Back in Numberland, One carries a basket of golden apples）*

**One（一号）:** I brought golden apples for everyone! / 我给大家带了金苹果！

*（一号分苹果：1 个、2 个、4 个、8 个 / One shares the apples: 1, 2, 4, 8）*

**One（一号）:** One apple, two apples, four apples, eight apples! / 一个苹果、两个苹果、四个苹果、八个苹果！

**All（大家）:** 🎵 Halve eight is four, halve four is two, / 🎵 八减半是四，四减半是二，

🎵 Halve two is one — now you can too! / 🎵 二减半是一——你也学会啦！

**One（一号）:** Doubling and halving — double trouble, double fun! / 翻倍和减半——翻倍的麻烦，翻倍的快乐！

*（大家吃着金苹果，笑着谢幕 / Everyone munches golden apples and waves goodbye）*

**All（大家）:** Bye-bye! / 再见！
