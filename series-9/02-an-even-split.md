# 第 2 集 Ep 2：An Even Split｜均匀分割

**剧情简介 Synopsis:** 无穷大要把一堆方块平分成两份，只有偶数才分得一样多。 / Infinity splits a pile of blocks into two equal halves—only even numbers divide evenly.

**角色 Characters:** 五号 Five、六号 Six、七号 Seven、八号 Eight、十二 Twelve、十四 Fourteen、十六 Sixteen、无穷大 Infinaughty

**剧本 Script:**

*（一堆方块被一道光分成左右两半 / A beam of light divides a pile of blocks in two）*

**Infinaughty（无穷大）:** Split them evenly—no leftovers allowed! / 要平分成两半——不许有剩！

**Six（六号）:** Six is three and three. Even split! / 六是三加三，分得均匀！

**Eight（八号）:** Eight is four and four. Perfect! / 八是四加四，完美！

**Twelve（十二）:** Six and six for me. / 我这边是六和六。

**Fourteen（十四）:** Seven and seven—still even! / 七加七——依然是偶数！

**Five（五号）:** Five gives two and two... with one left over. / 五分成二和二……还剩一个。

**Seven（七号）:** And I have one left over too. / 我也剩下一个。

**Infinaughty（无穷大）:** Odd numbers always leave one behind. / 奇数总会剩下一个。

**All（大家）:** Two equal halves: that's an even split! / 两份一样多：这就是均匀分割！
