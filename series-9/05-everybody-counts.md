# 第 5 集 Ep 5：Everybody Counts｜人人都来数

**剧情简介 Synopsis:** 从一数到五十的大合唱，每个数字都来报到，无穷大在最后加上一个大大的拥抱。 / A grand count from one to fifty invites every number to join, with infinity adding one last hug.

**角色 Characters:** 一号 One 到五十 Fifty、无穷大 Infinaughty、数字小球 Numberblobs

**剧本 Script:**

*（数字乐园的所有方块排成一条长龙 / Every Numberblock in Numberland lines up in one long row）*

**One（一号）:** 🎵 One, two, three—come count with me! / 🎵 一、二、三——跟我一起数！

**Ten（十号）:** 🎵 Four, five, six, seven, eight, nine, ten! / 🎵 四、五、六、七、八、九、十！

**Twenty（二十）:** 🎵 Eleven to twenty, again and again! / 🎵 十一到二十，一遍又一遍！

**Thirty（三十）:** 🎵 Twenty-one to thirty, steady and clear! / 🎵 二十一到三十，稳稳又清楚！

**Forty（四十）:** 🎵 Thirty-one to forty, loud and strong! / 🎵 三十一到四十，响亮又有力！

**Fifty（五十）:** 🎵 Forty-one to fifty—that's our song! / 🎵 四十一到五十——这就是我们的歌！

**Infinaughty（无穷大）:** And after fifty comes fifty-one... and on, and on! / 五十之后还有五十一……一直数下去！

*（全场一起拍手，节拍越来越快 / The whole crowd claps as the beat grows faster）*

**Numberblobs（数字小球）:** 🎵 Everybody counts, from the first to the last! / 🎵 人人都来数，从第一个到最后一个！

**All（大家）:** One to fifty, loud and proud: everybody counts! / 从一到五十，响亮又自豪：人人都来数！
