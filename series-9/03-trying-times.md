# 第 3 集 Ep 3：Trying Times｜艰难时刻

**剧情简介 Synopsis:** 乘法表接连出错，大伙儿一路试到天黑，把难记的倍数重新练熟。 / The times tables keep tripping everyone up, so the gang tries again until every multiple clicks.

**角色 Characters:** 八号 Eight、二十三 Twenty-three、二十四 Twenty-four、二十五 Twenty-five、二十六 Twenty-six、二十七 Twenty-seven、二十八 Twenty-eight、三十五 Thirty-five、三十七 Thirty-seven、三十九 Thirty-nine、四十五 Forty-five、四十六 Forty-six、四十八 Forty-eight、无穷大 Infinaughty

**剧本 Script:**

*（时钟滴答作响，乘法表挂歪在墙上 / A clock ticks and the times tables hang crooked）*

**Infinaughty（无穷大）:** Try again—every multiple needs practice! / 再试一次——每个倍数都要练！

**Twenty-four（二十四）:** Three eighths are twenty-four. / 三乘八等于二十四。

**Twenty-five（二十五）:** Five fives are twenty-five. / 五乘五等于二十五。

**Twenty-seven（二十七）:** Three nines are twenty-seven. / 三乘九等于二十七。

**Thirty-five（三十五）:** Five sevens are thirty-five. / 五乘七等于三十五。

**Forty-five（四十五）:** Five nines are forty-five—got it this time! / 五乘九等于四十五——这次记住了！

*（时钟敲响，挂表全部摆正 / The clock chimes and every table straightens）*

**Forty-eight（四十八）:** Six eights are forty-eight. / 六乘八等于四十八。

**All（大家）:** Trying times make sure times! / 艰难时刻，练出把握！
