### 📖 绘本简介 Synopsis

**中文:** 跟随三个充满好奇心的小伙伴漫步在郁郁葱葱的树林里，展开一场趣味盎然的大自然寻宝之旅。从像魔法棒一样的弯树枝、粗糙的树皮拓印，到旋转落下的“小直升机”种子和美丽的松果，孩子们一边观察寻宝，一边探索树木生长、自我保护以及林中生灵和谐共处的自然科学奥秘，最后还学会了爱护自然、文明寻宝的环保小准则。

**English:** Join three adventurous children on a delightful nature walk through the woods as they discover natural wonders and treasures, from curly sticks and textured bark to fluttering helicopter seeds and pine cones. Along the way, fascinating nature facts reveal how trees grow, protect themselves, and support forest wildlife, concluding with gentle guidelines on nature conservation.

### 🌿 大自然寻宝清单 Natural Treasures Found

- **A curly stick (一根弯弯的树枝)**: 在林间地面上找到，挥舞起来就像一根神奇的魔法棒。 / Found on the forest floor, perfect to wave like a magic wand.
- **Scratchy, lumpy bark (凹凸粗糙的树皮)**: 纹理斑驳的树皮，非常适合拿纸和蜡笔在上面做拓印。 / Tree bark with rich textures, perfect for making crayon rubbings on paper.
- **Fallen leaves (掉落的树叶)**: 各式各样的落叶，有的能扮成滑稽的兔耳朵，有的只留下像蕾丝般精美的叶脉。 / Fallen tree leaves of various shapes, some making funny bunny ears and others revealing delicate lace skeletons.
- **Helicopter seeds (Winged seeds / Samaras) (像直升机一样的翅果种子)**: 带有小翅膀的种子，随风飘落时像一架架小巧玲珑的直升机。 / Wing-shaped seeds that spin through the air like tiny helicopters on the wind.
- **A pine cone (一颗松果)**: 木质鳞片张开、形似花瓣的松果，里面小心地保护着树木的种子。 / A cone with open wooden scales spread like flower petals, which houses tree seeds.
- **A stripy, swirly snail shell (带条纹旋涡的空蜗牛壳)**: 在枯木旁找到的带漂亮旋涡花纹的空蜗牛壳。 / An empty shell left behind on the forest floor with beautiful spiral patterns.

### 📜 中英双语对照全文 Bilingual Script

#### 📍 1. Walking into the Woods & The Curly Stick ｜ 1. 漫步树林与神奇弯树枝

**EN:** Follow me, I know the way. We're walking through the woods today.  
**CN:** 跟着我来吧，我认得路！今天我们要穿越大树林去漫步。

**EN:** Look what I found! A curly stick to wave like a magic wand.  
**CN:** 瞧，我找到了什么！一根弯弯的小树枝，挥起来就像神奇的魔法棒。

**EN:** Can you also see: 1 signpost to show the way, 2 butterflies fluttering, 3 bright yellow flowers?  
**CN:** 你还能看到这些吗：1块指路的路标，2只翩翩起舞的蝴蝶，3朵明艳的黄花？

**EN:** A tree's branches and leaves are called its crown. Crowns grow in different shapes and sizes.  
**CN:** 树木的枝丫和叶子统称为树冠。树冠的大小和形状各不相同。

**EN:** Some are tall and pointed, and some spread out widely.  
**CN:** 有的高耸尖挺，有的枝叶伸展，宽阔如盖。

**EN:** Sometimes, trees have other plants growing on them.  
**CN:** 有时候，树干上还会寄生其他植物。

**EN:** Look out for powdery green or orange algae, grey splodges of lichen, or cushions of furry green moss.  
**CN:** 仔细找找看：有粉末状的绿藻或橙藻，有一团团灰色的地衣，还有垫子般毛茸茸的绿青苔。

**EN:** Tree shapes to spot: Aspen: A long, thin trunk with leaves high up.  
**CN:** 观察树木的形态：欧洲山杨：树干细长挺拔，树叶长得高高的。

**EN:** Pine: Pointed like a space rocket.  
**CN:** 松树：尖尖挺立，就像一枚太空火箭。

**EN:** Beech: Puffy like a rain cloud.  
**CN:** 山毛榉：蓬松茂密，就像一朵蓄满雨水的大云朵。

**EN:** Oak: A thick trunk you can stretch your arms around.  
**CN:** 橡树：粗壮的树干，张开双臂才能环抱得过来。

#### 📍 2. Looking at Bark ｜ 2. 近看树皮拓印与树皮的秘密

**EN:** Look at the trees up close. Which ones will we like the most?  
**CN:** 走近瞧瞧这些树，哪一棵我们最喜欢？

**EN:** Look what I found! Some scratchy, lumpy bark to make a rubbing on.  
**CN:** 瞧，我发现了什么！凹凸粗糙的树皮，正好可以印出一张漂亮的拓画。

**EN:** Can you also see: 1 twiggy bird's nest, 2 squirrels racing, 3 busy bumblebees?  
**CN:** 你还能看到这些吗：1个枯枝筑起的小鸟窝，2只追逐奔跑的松鼠，3只忙忙碌碌的大蜜蜂？

**EN:** Bark is similar to our own skin because it protects the inside of the tree, keeping it safe from hot sun or icy wind.  
**CN:** 树皮就像我们的皮肤一样，守护着树木的内部，遮蔽酷暑烈日，抵御刺骨寒风。

**EN:** Bark also protects trees from harmful insects.  
**CN:** 树皮还能保护大树免受害虫的侵害。

**EN:** Some trees even make sticky resin that seeps out of bark like dollops of glue. It is full of chemicals that help to keep the insects away.  
**CN:** 有些树甚至会分泌出黏糊糊的树脂，像一团团胶水般从树皮渗出。树脂里含有特殊的化学物质，能把害虫驱赶走。

**EN:** It could make you feel ill too, so it's best not to touch it.  
**CN:** 接触树脂可能会让你身体不适，所以最好别碰它哦。

**EN:** Trees sometimes have holes in their bark where a branch once grew. Animals such as squirrels and birds use the holes as cosy hiding places.  
**CN:** 树皮上长过树枝的地方有时会留下树洞。松鼠和小鸟等动物会把这些洞当作舒适温暖的藏身小窝。

**EN:** Bark can be spotty or stripy, cracked or scaly. Some bark even looks like wallpaper peeling off an old wall.  
**CN:** 树皮有带斑点的，有带条纹的，有的布满裂纹，有的层叠如鳞。有的树皮看起来就像从旧墙上剥落的壁纸。

**EN:** Animals like deer sometimes nibble on bark. It makes a tasty snack for them, but it can damage the tree too.  
**CN:** 像小鹿这样的动物有时会啃食树皮。对它们来说这是美味的零食，但却可能会伤害大树。

**EN:** Types of bark to spot: Smooth with tiny grooves. Shapes like big scales. Lines that go from side to side. Lines going up and down.  
**CN:** 观察树皮的纹理：光滑且带着细细凹槽的；形如大鱼鳞的；横向延伸的条纹；纵向延伸的直纹。

#### 📍 3. Leaves and Leaf Shapes ｜ 3. 飘落的树叶与叶片的奥秘

**EN:** Finding treasure from the trees, blown down by the rustling breeze.  
**CN:** 寻找大树掉落的宝贝，那是在沙沙微风中轻轻落下的馈赠。

**EN:** Look what I found! Two leaves that make funny bunny ears!  
**CN:** 瞧，我找到了什么！两片树叶，正好可以扮成滑稽的兔耳朵！

**EN:** Can you also see: 1 robin hopping, 2 lacy spiderwebs, 3 little purple flowers?  
**CN:** 你还能看到这些吗：1只蹦蹦跳跳的知更鸟，2张蕾丝般的蜘蛛网，3朵娇小的紫色小花？

**EN:** Leaves are one of the most important parts of a tree. They use the sunlight to make a sugary food that helps the tree to grow.  
**CN:** 叶子是大树最重要的部分之一。它们利用阳光制造出含糖的养分，帮助大树茁壮成长。

**EN:** If you hold a thin leaf up to the light, you might be able to see lots of tiny tunnels inside it.  
**CN:** 如果你把一片薄薄的树叶对着光线看，可能会看到叶片内部有许多细小的“通道”。

**EN:** They carry water to the leaf, and they take the food that the leaf makes back to the tree.  
**CN:** 这些叶脉将水分输送给叶片，再把叶片制造出的养料送回大树体内。

**EN:** Some trees have needles for leaves. The needles stay on their branches all year round, and these trees are called evergreen.  
**CN:** 有些树的叶子是针状的。针叶一年四季都留在枝头上，这类树木被称为常绿树。

**EN:** Some trees lose their leaves in autumn. The leaves change to red, orange, yellow or brown before they fall. Trees grow new green leaves in the spring.  
**CN:** 有些树会在秋天落叶。叶子在掉落前会变成红色、橙色、黄色或褐色。到了春天，大树又会抽出一片片翠绿的新叶。

**EN:** Leaf shapes to spot: Oak: A leaf that has wavy edges like a wiggly worm.  
**CN:** 观察树叶的形状：橡树叶：边缘呈波浪状，像一条扭来扭去的小毛虫。

**EN:** Birch: A leaf that has jagged edges like the teeth of a zip.  
**CN:** 桦树叶：边缘呈锯齿状，就像拉链上的细齿。

**EN:** Maple skeleton: A leaf that has lost its covering, so it looks as if it is made of lace.  
**CN:** 枫叶叶脉标本：叶肉已经脱落，看起来就像蕾丝织成的一样精美。

**EN:** Larch: Tufts of needles on a stalk.  
**CN:** 落叶松：细茎上一簇簇柔软的针叶。

**EN:** Ash: Several leaves fanning out from one stem.  
**CN:** 欧洲白蜡树：几片叶子从一根叶柄上呈扇形散开。

**EN:** Beech: A single leaf with straight edges, shaped like an eye.  
**CN:** 山毛榉叶：边缘平直的单片叶子，形状像一只明亮的小眼睛。

**EN:** Pine: A stalk lined with needles.  
**CN:** 松针：一根排列着针叶的小枝。

#### 📍 4. Under the Tree & Helicopter Seeds ｜ 4. 树底寻觅与直升机种子

**EN:** Hunting underneath a tree. What small secrets can we see?  
**CN:** 在大树底下仔细找寻，我们能发现什么隐秘的小秘密？

**EN:** Look what I found! A seed like a tiny helicopter.  
**CN:** 瞧，我找到了什么！一颗像微型直升机一样的种子。

**EN:** Can you also see: 1 big blackbird, 2 crawling centipedes, 3 tiny wild strawberries?  
**CN:** 你还能看到这些吗：1只黑色的大鸫鸟，2只爬行的蜈蚣，3颗红红的野生小草莓？

**EN:** Some trees grow their seeds inside hard nuts. When they are ready, the nuts will fall off the tree, and you can find the seeds hidden inside.  
**CN:** 有些树把种子包裹在坚硬的坚果壳里。成熟时坚果从树上落下，你就能在壳里找到种子。

**EN:** Some trees grow their seeds inside soft fruit, such as apples and pears.  
**CN:** 有些树把种子包裹在多汁柔软的果实里，比如苹果和梨。

**EN:** Some trees grow their seeds inside little wing shapes that blow away like tiny helicopters in the wind.  
**CN:** 有些树将种子长成小翅膀的形状，在风中飞舞时就像一架架小巧的直升机。

**EN:** In the autumn, trees make seeds that fall to the ground. The seeds might one day grow into new trees.  
**CN:** 秋天里，树木结出的种子纷纷落到地上。也许有一天，这些种子就会长成新的大树。

**EN:** Nuts and nutcases to spot: Sweet chestnut: A nutcase with sharp spines. Hazelnut: A shiny, dark nutcase.  
**CN:** 观察坚果与坚果外壳：欧洲栗（板栗）：长满尖刺的外壳；榛子：深色而有光泽的果壳。

**EN:** Horse chestnut: A spiky nutcase with seeds inside. Beechnut: A small nutcase open like a star.  
**CN:** 欧洲七叶树（马栗）：带刺的外壳，里面藏着种子；山毛榉坚果：像小星星一样裂开的小果壳。

**EN:** Acorn: A small nut in a cup that might have a hole in it where an animal has chewed it.  
**CN:** 橡果：戴着小帽子的小坚果，上面可能还留着小动物啃咬过的小孔洞。

#### 📍 5. Woodland Shade & Pine Cones ｜ 5. 树林阴凉处与神奇松果

**EN:** Searching in the woodland shade, finding things the trees have made.  
**CN:** 在树林幽凉的树荫下寻觅，寻找大树创造出的神奇物件。

**EN:** Look what I found! A pine cone with its scales stretched open like petals.  
**CN:** 瞧，我找到了什么！一颗松果，鳞片舒展开来，就像盛开的花瓣。

**EN:** Can you also see: 1 bird box where birds can nest, 2 black crows, 3 pink flowers?  
**CN:** 你还能看到这些吗：1个供鸟儿筑巢的人工鸟箱，2只黑乌鸦，3朵粉红色的花？

**EN:** Trees that have needles grow hard cones to protect their seeds.  
**CN:** 长着针叶的树会长出坚硬的球果，用来保护它们的种子。

**EN:** The seeds are tucked safely under the scales of the cone, instead of inside nuts or soft fruit.  
**CN:** 它们的种子被严严实实地藏在球果的鳞片下，而不是包裹在坚果壳或多汁果肉里。

**EN:** The cone scales shut tightly to protect the seeds when the weather is cold or damp.  
**CN:** 当天气阴冷潮湿时，松果的鳞片会紧紧闭合，保护里面的种子不受侵害。

**EN:** The smallest cones are no bigger than your thumb, but the biggest ones can be as big as rugby balls.  
**CN:** 最小的球果还没有你的大拇指大，但最大的球果甚至可以和橄榄球一样大！

**EN:** When the weather gets warm and dry, the cone scales open, and the seeds fall out, ready to grow into new trees.  
**CN:** 等到天气变得温暖干燥，球果的鳞片便会重新张开，种子落向地面，准备生根发芽长成新的大树。

**EN:** Cone shapes to spot: A big, round cone with open scales. A long, thin cone. A bunch of mini cones. A short, fat cone.  
**CN:** 观察球果的形状：鳞片张开的圆圆大松果；细细长长的球果；一簇紧挨着的迷你小球果；矮矮胖胖的球果。

#### 📍 6. Dead Logs & Snail Shells ｜ 6. 枯木与带花纹的蜗牛壳

**EN:** Spotting logs with holes inside, where little creatures like to hide.  
**CN:** 寻找带树洞的枯倒木，小动物们最爱在里面躲藏。

**EN:** Look what I found! A stripy, swirly snail shell.  
**CN:** 瞧，我找到了什么！一个带着条纹旋涡的蜗牛壳。

**EN:** Can you also see: 1 slimy black slug, 2 spotty ladybirds, 3 shiny black beetles?  
**CN:** 你还能看到这些吗：1只黏糊糊的黑蛞蝓，2只带斑点的瓢虫，3只油亮发光的黑甲虫？

**EN:** A snail carries its own hiding place on its back. It can curl up and disappear inside its shell.  
**CN:** 蜗牛的背上就背着自己的藏身小家。遇到危险时，它可以缩成一团藏进壳里。

**EN:** Be sure to only collect empty shells, so you don't harm any little snails.  
**CN:** 捡拾的时候一定要确保收集的是空的空壳，千万不要伤害到活蜗牛哦。

**EN:** Snails and slugs eat leaves and bark, so the woods are a great place for them to find a meal.  
**CN:** 蜗牛和蛞蝓以树叶和树皮为食，因此茂密的树林是它们大饱口福的好地方。

**EN:** Beetles might crawl inside a dead log and munch up the old wood.  
**CN:** 甲虫常常爬进腐朽的木头里，大口啃食陈旧的木质纤维。

**EN:** Animals munch up dead wood and leaves to help keep the woods clean. They are like tiny recycling trucks, reusing the wood for their food.  
**CN:** 动物们啃食枯木和落叶，帮助林地保持干净清爽。它们就像一辆辆小小的垃圾回收车，将枯木再循环利用，变成自己的食物。

**EN:** Lots of woodland animals hunt other smaller creatures to eat. No wonder minibeasts like to hide!  
**CN:** 林子里许多动物都会捕食比自己更小的生物。难怪这些小虫子们都喜欢把自己藏起来！

**EN:** Snail shells to spot: A tiny shell that's round and flat. A shell with dark and light stripes. A shell that looks see-through, like glass. A shell that's shaped like a cone.  
**CN:** 观察蜗牛壳的外观：圆圆扁扁的小壳；有深浅条纹相间的壳；像玻璃一样晶莹通透的壳；形状像圆锥体的尖尖壳。

#### 📍 7. Homeward Bound & Responsible Treasure Collecting ｜ 7. 满载而归与文明寻宝守则

**EN:** Goodbye, woods. We're homeward bound. Look at all the things we found!  
**CN:** 再见啦，大树林！我们要回家去喽。快看我们找到的所有宝贝！

**EN:** Treasure!  
**CN:** 全是大自然的大宝藏！

**EN:** As you collect, be thoughtful, too. Bring no harm with what you do.  
**CN:** 寻宝采集的时候，心里也要多替大自然着想，千万别让你的举动带来伤害。

**EN:** When you're exploring the woods, only pick things up from the ground.  
**CN:** 在树林里探险时，只能捡拾掉落在地面上的东西。

**EN:** Never pick things from trees or flowers, or do anything that would harm animals.  
**CN:** 千万不要去采摘活树上的枝叶、攀折花朵，或者做出伤害小动物的事情。

**EN:** That way, the woods will stay beautiful and healthy for your next visit.  
**CN:** 只有这样，等到你下一次拜访时，大森林才会依然美丽如初、充满生机！
