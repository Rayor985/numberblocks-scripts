### 📖 绘本简介 Synopsis

**中文:** 跟着小朋友一起走进公园，开启一场充满好奇与欢笑的大自然寻宝之旅！从紧紧闭合的松果、被小虫啃过的绿叶，到飘落的芳香玫瑰花瓣、枝头欢唱的小鸟、忙碌的微型小动物以及苍翠古老的大树，这本由英国国家名胜古迹信托（National Trust）推荐的自然启蒙图画书，带领孩子们在观察、数数与游戏中，认识生态奥秘，学会爱护自然环境。

**English:** Join a playful, observant adventure through the park to discover the wonders of nature. From bumpy pine cones and nibbled leaves to fragrant rose petals, singing birds, busy minibeasts, and ancient trees, this delightful National Trust picture book encourages young children to explore, observe, count, and cherish outdoor green spaces.

### 🌿 大自然寻宝清单 Natural Treasures Found

- **A Pine Cone (松果)**: 表面布满凸起的鳞片，潮湿时紧紧闭合，干燥时舒展张开以散播种子。 / Its knobbly scales are locked tightly shut when damp and open when dry to scatter seeds.
- **A Nibbled Leaf (被啃咬过的绿叶)**: 边缘留下一圈小齿痕的翠绿树叶，证明它是毛毛虫或蜗牛的美餐。 / A big green leaf nibbled along the edges, showing it was a delicious meal for a caterpillar or snail.
- **Rose Petals (玫瑰花瓣)**: 轻柔芬芳的玫瑰花瓣，随风飘落铺在草地上。 / Soft, sweet-smelling flower petals that have gently floated down to the ground.
- **Bird Footprints (小鸟脚印)**: 小鸟踩在湿润泥土上留下的一串细巧整齐的小足迹。 / A neat line of tiny claw footprints left in the soft earth or mud by a walking bird.
- **A Tree Bark Rubbing (树皮拓印画)**: 用纸张紧贴凹凸不平的粗糙树干，用蜡笔拓印出的精美天然纹理图案。 / A creative, textured rubbing picture made by pressing paper onto bumpy tree bark and rubbing with a crayon.
- **The Perfect Stick (最棒的小树枝)**: 长短粗细正正合适的小枯枝，是在沙池里画画写字的天然画笔。 / A fallen twig just the right size and shape to sketch pictures and patterns in the sandpit.

**CN:** 今天让我们去公园探险吧，一路上会发现许多大自然的小秘密！

**CN:** 快瞧我找到了什么！一个松果！它凸起的小鳞片正紧紧闭合着呢。

**CN:** 公园里的植物为我们带来缤纷的色彩与清凉的树荫，也为动物们提供了温暖的家园和丰盛的美食。

**EN:** Leaves absorb—soak up—sunlight and a gas called carbon dioxide from the air, and water from the ground.  
**CN:** 树叶会吸收——也就是汲取——阳光、空气中一种叫做二氧化碳的气体，以及土壤中的水分。

**CN:** 接着，它们把这些养分调配在一起，给自己制作甜甜的糖分食物。

**EN:** Plants have all sorts of different leaves. Some are flat, while others are shaped like tiny needles.  
**CN:** 植物的叶子千姿百态。有的扁平宽大，有的则像一根根细小的针。

**CN:** 它们有的有你的手掌那么大，有的却只有指甲盖那么小。

**EN:** Plants make seeds, which drop to the ground to make new plants.  
**CN:** 植物会结出种子，落在泥土里长出新的植株。

**EN:** Some plants make their seeds inside cones.  
**CN:** 有些植物是在球果里面孕育种子的。

**EN:** When it's damp, the cone stays closed tight. When it's dry, it opens up to scatter the seeds.  
**CN:** 天气潮湿时，球果紧紧闭拢；天气干燥时，它就会悄悄张开，把种子播撒到四方。

**EN:** Wild park plants to spot:  
**CN:** 在公园里找找这些野生植物吧：

**EN:** Daisies and dandelions. They can be seen from springtime until the end of autumn.  
**CN:** 雏菊与蒲公英：从明媚的春天一直到深秋，都能见到它们可爱的身影。

**EN:** Ribwort plantain. A flower with fuzzy, pointed seed heads growing on a long stalk.  
**CN:** 长叶车前：细长的花茎上顶着毛茸茸、尖尖的种子头。

**EN:** Nettles. Nettles have tiny stinging hairs under their leaves and on their stems.  
**CN:** 荨麻：在它们的叶背和茎干上长着细小的刺毛，摸起来会刺痛哦。

**EN:** Who will be the first to spy a beetle or a butterfly?  
**CN:** 谁会第一个发现甲虫或蝴蝶的身影？

**EN:** Look what I found! A big green leaf that's been nibbled around the edges!  
**CN:** 瞧我找到了什么！一片边缘被咬得坑坑洼洼的大绿叶！

**EN:** Can you also see: one silky spider's web, two little ladybirds, three blackbirds?  
**CN:** 你还能看到这些吗：一张丝滑的蜘蛛网、两只小小的七星瓢虫、三只乌鸫鸟？

**EN:** The park makes a great home for minibeasts, such as snails, woodlice and insects.  
**CN:** 公园是蜗牛、潮虫和各种昆虫等微型小动物的美好家园。

**EN:** Once warm weather comes, they get busy hunting for food or laying eggs.  
**CN:** 暖和的天气一来，它们就忙着四处觅食、产卵繁衍。

**EN:** If you see a nibbled leaf, it has probably been a meal for a caterpillar or a snail.  
**CN:** 如果你看到一片被咬出缺口的叶子，那它很可能是毛毛虫或蜗牛的一顿美餐。

**EN:** Caterpillars eat so much that they can grow twice as big in just a few days.  
**CN:** 毛毛虫的胃口可大了，才短短几天功夫，个头就能长到原来的两倍大！

**EN:** Some beetles eat dead leaves and wood, so you're most likely to find them on the ground.  
**CN:** 有些甲虫喜欢吃枯叶和朽木，所以你最容易在地面上发现它们。

**EN:** Other beetles, such as ladybirds, hunt for small insects on plants.  
**CN:** 还有一些甲虫，比如瓢虫，会在植物上捕食微小的害虫。

**EN:** A week or two after it hatches, a caterpillar will make itself a covering called a chrysalis or cocoon.  
**CN:** 毛毛虫孵化后的一两周，就会给自己织一个保护壳，叫做蛹或茧。

**EN:** Eventually, it will hatch as a butterfly or a moth.  
**CN:** 最终，它会羽化破茧，蜕变成美丽的蝴蝶或飞蛾。

**EN:** Ants live in a big group called a colony. They often nest underground, digging out tunnels in the soil.  
**CN:** 蚂蚁过着群居生活，组成庞大的蚁群。它们常常在地下筑巢，在泥土里挖掘复杂的隧道。

**EN:** Minibeast homes to spot:  
**CN:** 找找小虫子们的家吧：

**EN:** Spider's web. Made of sticky silk to catch tiny, tasty bugs.  
**CN:** 蜘蛛网：用粘粘的蛛丝织成，专门用来捕捉美味可口的小飞虫。

**EN:** Bug hotel. Made by humans with safe hiding places for minibeasts.  
**CN:** 昆虫旅馆：人类用天然材料搭建的庇护所，为小虫子们提供安全的藏身之处。

**EN:** Anthill. A hole in the ground with a mini mound of fine soil on top.  
**CN:** 蚁穴：地面上的小洞口，顶上隆起一圈由细腻泥土堆成的小土丘。

#### 📍 3. Flower Friends and Sweet Rose Petals ｜ 3. 鲜花伙伴与芬芳玫瑰花瓣

**EN:** Flowers are nodding in the breeze, visited by bugs and bees.  
**CN:** 花儿在微风中轻轻点头，小虫和蜜蜂忙着前来拜访。

**EN:** Look what I found! Soft, sweet-smelling rose petals. They have floated to the ground.  
**CN:** 瞧我找到了什么！柔柔滑滑、香气扑鼻的玫瑰花瓣，随风轻轻落在了地上。

**EN:** Can you also see: one watering can, two blue butterflies, three busy bees?  
**CN:** 你还能看到这些吗：一个浇水壶、两只蓝蝴蝶、三只忙碌的小蜜蜂？

**EN:** Some plants can only make seeds if their dusty pollen is moved from one flower to another. This is called pollination.  
**CN:** 有些植物必须把花粉从一朵花传到另一朵花上才能结出种子，这个神奇的过程叫做授粉。

**EN:** Insects such as bees, wasps and butterflies land on flowers to collect sweet liquid called nectar.  
**CN:** 蜜蜂、黄蜂和蝴蝶等昆虫停留在花朵上，吸食甜滋滋的花蜜。

**EN:** Pollen sticks to them, and they take it to the next plant they visit.  
**CN:** 花粉会粘在它们的身上，随着它们飞到下一株植物上完成传粉。

**EN:** Seeds are sometimes hidden inside the fruit or berries of a plant. These make a tasty snack for mice, foxes and squirrels.  
**CN:** 植物的种子有时藏在果实或浆果里面，这些是小老鼠、狐狸和松鼠最爱吃的美味零食。

**EN:** Once the plants have made seeds, some of the park animals help to spread them around by eating them and then pooping them out.  
**CN:** 植物结出种子后，公园里的一些小动物吃下浆果，然后通过便便把种子排到各处，帮植物播撒新生命！

**EN:** The plants and the creatures in the park are all linked together, helping to keep it a healthy, beautiful place.  
**CN:** 公园里的植物与生灵紧紧相依、休戚与共，共同守护着这片健康美丽的大自然。

**EN:** Humans can help, too, by looking after the park.  
**CN:** 我们人类也能出一份力，用心爱护公园里的一草一木。

**EN:** Spot ways flowers attract insects:  
**CN:** 看看花朵是怎样吸引昆虫的：

**EN:** Flowers sometimes give off a perfume to bring insects in.  
**CN:** 花朵有时会散发出迷人的芬芳，引诱昆虫飞来。

**EN:** Lines on petals to guide insects to nectar and pollen in the middle of the flower.  
**CN:** 花瓣上的条纹像小跑道，指引昆虫通往花朵中央的花蜜和花粉。

**EN:** Coloured circles on petals guide an insect into the middle of the flower.  
**CN:** 花瓣上鲜艳的环形斑纹，如同靶心一般引导昆虫停靠中央。

**EN:** Bees particularly like visiting blue, purple and yellow flowers.  
**CN:** 蜜蜂尤其喜欢探访蓝色、紫色和黄色的花朵。

#### 📍 4. Bird Watching and Tiny Footprints ｜ 4. 观察飞鸟与小巧爪印

**EN:** We're counting all the birds we see: duck, swan, pigeon, one, two, three!  
**CN:** 我们正在数着看到的每一只小鸟：鸭子、天鹅、鸽子，一、二、三！

**EN:** Look what I found! A line of little footprints made by a bird.  
**CN:** 瞧我找到了什么！一串小鸟踩出的小爪印！

**EN:** Can you also see: one little frog, two bird nesting boxes, three hovering dragonflies?  
**CN:** 你还能看到这些吗：一只小青蛙、两个人工鸟巢箱、三只盘旋振翅的蜻蜓？

**EN:** Birds, big and small, visit the park to find food. Some of them even live in the park and raise their families there.  
**CN:** 大大小小的鸟儿来到公园觅食。有的甚至长居于此，在这里安家养育小宝宝。

**EN:** You might see nest boxes fixed to the trees. They are for little birds who like to build their nests in hidden nooks and crannies.  
**CN:** 你可能会看到树干上固定着人工鸟巢箱，那是为喜欢在隐蔽树洞里筑巢的小鸟准备的温馨小屋。

**EN:** Water birds, such as ducks and swans, live in ponds and lakes. They dip down underwater to find their favourite foods: fish, pondweed, snails and frogs. Yum!  
**CN:** 水鸟（比如野鸭和天鹅）生活在池塘与湖泊中。它们一头扎进水下寻找最爱吃的美食：小鱼、水草、水螺还有青蛙。真香呀！

**EN:** Small birds sing in the trees and bushes. They send messages to other birds: 'This patch of the park is mine, go away!' or 'Listen to how well I sing, come and nest with me!'  
**CN:** 小巧的鸣禽在树梢和灌木丛中欢唱，向同伴传递心声：“这片地盘归我啦，快走开！”或是“听我唱得多动听，快来跟我一起筑巢吧！”

**EN:** Bigger birds, such as ducks and swans, don't sing, but they do quack, honk or squawk. They might be saying, 'Watch out for danger!' or 'Hey Mum, I'm over here!'  
**CN:** 体型较大的鸟类（如鸭子和天鹅）虽然不会婉转鸣唱，但它们会呱呱叫、引吭高鸣或高声嘎嘎叫。它们可能在喊：“小心危险！”或者“妈妈，我在这儿呢！”

**EN:** Park birds to spot:  
**CN:** 在公园里观察这些鸟儿的姿态吧：

**EN:** A bird with wide wings stretched out to catch the breeze.  
**CN:** 展翅高飞、迎风滑翔的鸟儿。

**EN:** A bird with toes tightly curled round a branch.  
**CN:** 脚趾紧紧抓住树枝栖息的鸟儿。

**EN:** A bird pecking on the ground for worms, bugs or seeds.  
**CN:** 在草地上啄食小虫、蚯蚓或种子的鸟儿。

**EN:** A bird paddling in the water with its webbed feet.  
**CN:** 用脚蹼在水波中自在划水的水鸟。

#### 📍 5. Ancient Trees and Bark Rubbings ｜ 5. 参天古树与树皮拓印

**EN:** We love the trees, some thick, some thin. It's fun to stroke their bumpy skin!  
**CN:** 我们喜爱大树，有的粗壮，有的纤细。抚摸它们凹凸不平的粗糙树皮真好玩！

**EN:** Look what I found! A clever way to make a picture of a tree bark!  
**CN:** 瞧我找到了什么！一个拓印树皮纹理的绝妙画画好办法！

**EN:** Can you also see: one fox, two picnic tables, three signs?  
**CN:** 你还能看到这些吗：一只小狐狸、两张野餐桌、三块指路指示牌？

**EN:** Lots of creatures live in trees. At the bottom, there might be a mouse burrow or a fox den.  
**CN:** 大树是许多动物的乐园。在树根底部，可能有小老鼠的地洞或小狐狸的窝。

**EN:** Tiny insects, such as woodlice, ladybirds and snails, will be hiding in the bark, while birds might be nesting in the branches.  
**CN:** 潮虫、瓢虫和蜗牛等小生物会藏在树皮缝隙里，而鸟儿则在繁茂的枝桠间筑巢安家。

**EN:** Roots soak up water and goodness from the soil. The large root system of big trees helps to keep them stable in strong winds.  
**CN:** 树根源源不断地从土壤中吸取水分和养分。大树庞大的根系还能让它们在狂风中稳稳矗立。

**EN:** A tree's bark is a tough skin that protects it from harm. It's different from tree to tree: knobbly, smooth or even scaly.  
**CN:** 树皮是坚韧的外衣，保护树木免受外界伤害。每棵树的树皮都各具特色：有的疙疙瘩瘩，有的光滑如砥，还有的布满层层鳞片。

**EN:** In some historic parks, there might be trees that are more than a hundred years old.  
**CN:** 在一些古老幽静的历史名园里，常常能看到树龄超过百年的参天大树。

**EN:** Trunks grow wider as trees get older, so look for the widest ones—they've probably been in the park the longest!  
**CN:** 随着年岁增长，树干会越来越粗壮。找找那些最粗的大树吧，它们陪伴公园的岁月大概最悠久了！

**EN:** Tree secrets to spot:  
**CN:** 找找大树隐藏的小秘密：

**EN:** Animals such as owls, squirrels or bats might live in tree holes.  
**CN:** 猫头鹰、松鼠或蝙蝠等小动物可能会住在隐秘的树洞里。

**EN:** Moss, algae and lichen grow on trees without harming them: spongy moss, grey lichen, green powdery algae.  
**CN:** 苔藓、藻类和地衣依附在树木上生长，却不会对大树造成任何伤害：海绵般松软的苔藓、灰灰的地衣、绿如粉末的微藻。

**EN:** Roots sometimes snake across the ground before they grow downwards.  
**CN:** 粗壮的树根有时会像长蛇一样蜿蜒盘踞在地面上，然后才深深扎入土壤底层。

#### 📍 6. The Playground and Nature's Games ｜ 6. 游乐场与大自然的游戏

**EN:** Magical mermaids and dolphins await. They're on the fountain near the gate.  
**CN:** 神奇的美人鱼和海豚正静静等待，它们就在大门旁的喷泉雕塑上嬉戏。

**CN:** 瞧我找到了什么！一根用来在沙地上画画的最棒小树枝！

**EN:** Can you also see: one squirrel, two ice creams, three recycling bins?  
**CN:** 你还能看到这些吗：一只小松鼠、两支冰淇淋、三个分类回收垃圾桶？

**CN:** 大公园里通常都建有游乐场。在那里你可以攀爬、荡秋千、挖沙子，甚至假装自己是一只公园里的小动物！

**EN:** Which park animal would you like to be? You could flutter like a pigeon or a butterfly, hop like a frog, buzz round like a bee, or be an expert climber like a squirrel!  
**CN:** 你想当哪只公园小动物呢？你可以像鸽子或蝴蝶一样扑棱起飞，像青蛙一样欢快蹦跳，像蜜蜂一样嗡嗡盘旋，或者像小松鼠一样成为攀爬小能手！

**EN:** Magical animals often live in parks. They might be made of stone, wood or iron. Look out for them on fountains, gates, roofs, and sometimes in playgrounds, too!  
**CN:** 公园里常藏着神奇的“魔法动物”。它们可能是用石头、木头或铁铸造雕刻的。在喷泉、大门、屋顶甚至游乐场里，留心找找它们的踪影吧！

**EN:** Look out for fluffy dandelion seed heads in the park, like pom-poms full of tiny seeds. Spot tiny wild daisies in the grass.  
**CN:** 找找公园里毛茸茸的蒲公英绒球吧，就像挂满小种子的绒球花；在草丛里寻找星星点点的野雏菊。

**EN:** Nature's playground:  
**CN:** 大自然的游乐场玩法：

**EN:** Dandelion game. Blow the seed head over and over, and count how many puffs it takes until all the seeds have gone: one, two, three.  
**CN:** 吹蒲公英游戏：一下又一下用力吹动蒲公英花球，数数看吹几口气才能把所有种子全吹走：一、二、三！

**EN:** Daisy chains. Pick daisies with some stalks on the ends. Make holes in the stalks with your fingernail, then thread the daisies together.  
**CN:** 雏菊手链：采摘带着一段细茎的雏菊，用指甲在茎上轻轻掐出小孔，再把花朵一朵接一朵串联起来吧。

#### 📍 7. Treasuring and Caring for the Park ｜ 7. 寻获宝藏与守护公园

**EN:** We've explored the park and run around. Look at all the things we've found!
