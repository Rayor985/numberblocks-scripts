📖 绘本故事简介
跟随充满好奇心的小朋友走进生机勃勃的大农场，开启一场大自然寻宝之旅！从挂在栅栏上的柔软羊毛簇、吹响草笛的草茎，到如天蓝色般的碎蛋壳和娇嫩的苹果花瓣，本书带领孩子们观察农场动物、野外生灵的家园与四季作物的生长，配有生动有趣的寻宝互动与爱护自然的环保提示。
Join a curious child on an outdoor adventure across a bustling farm! From discovering soft tufts of sheep's wool along the fences to making grass whistles and blowing dandelion seed clocks, young readers explore farm animals, wildlife habitats, seasonal crops, and the wonders of nature, complete with interactive spotting games and gentle conservation tips.

📍 Sheep in the Meadow · 草地上的绵羊
Today we're on the farm. Let's go! Past the gate where daisies grow.
今天我们去农场。出发啦！穿过雏菊盛开的木栅门。
Look what I found! A tuft of sheep's wool, soft as a woolly jumper.
快看我找到了什么！一簇小羊毛，像羊毛衫一样软绵绵。
Can you also see: One signpost to show the way, Two blue butterflies, Three curly white sheep?
你还能找到这些吗：一个指路的路标，两只蓝色的蝴蝶，三只卷卷白毛的绵羊？
A farm is a place for growing crops or raising animals.
农场是种植庄稼或饲养动物的地方。
Farmers usually live in a farmhouse with nearby fields where their animals graze and crops can grow.
农场主通常住在农舍里，农舍周围有田地，动物可以在那里吃草，庄稼也能茁壮成长。
Sheep have a thick, woolly coat called a fleece.
绵羊长着厚厚的羊毛外衣，叫做羊毛茸（fleece）。
Sometimes, if a sheep brushes past a fence, it can leave tiny tufts of its fleece behind.
有时候，绵羊擦过篱笆，就会在上面留下一小簇一小簇的羊毛。
Once a year, a sheep's thick fleece is shaved off and it might be made into wool for knitting.
每年会有一次，农夫给绵羊剪下厚厚的羊毛，它们会被纺成用来织毛衣的毛线。
A farm can be home to all kinds of animals.
农场是各种各样动物温暖的家。
Look out for sheep with black or white faces, long, curly horns, or extra long, shaggy coats.
找一找长着黑脸或白脸的绵羊、长而弯曲的羊角，或是格外蓬松的长毛大衣吧。
Cows can be black, brown, cream or white, or sometimes splodged with black like spilled ink.
奶牛有黑色的、棕色的、米色的或白色的，有时身上还带着像打翻了墨水般的黑斑块。
The breeds all have different names, like Holstein, Jersey or Highland.
不同品种有不同的名字，比如荷斯坦牛、娟姗牛或苏格兰高地牛。
Animal footprints to spot: Cow: two fat curved shapes like giant beans. Sheep: two long bunny ear shapes. Horse: a curved shape, the shape of the metal shoe on the horse's feet. Chicken: three pointy lines.
寻找动物脚印：奶牛：两个胖乎乎的弯曲印记，像大豆瓣。绵羊：两个长长的印记，像兔耳朵。马：弯弯的印记，那是马蹄铁的形状。小鸡：三条尖尖的细线。
📍 Chicks in the Pen · 鸡舍里的小鸡
Who's that in the pen? Cheeping chicks with mother hen.
圈栏里那是谁呀？是叽叽喳喳的小鸡和母鸡妈妈。
Look what I found! A fluffy chick feather, as yellow as the sun.
快看我找到了什么！一片毛茸茸的雏鸡羽毛，像太阳一样金黄。
Can you also see: One empty eggshell, Two chickens fast asleep, Three eggs that haven't hatched yet?
你还能找到这些吗：一个空蛋壳，两只熟睡的母鸡，三颗还没孵化的鸡蛋？
Most baby animals are born in springtime, when the weather is warmer and there's plenty of food for them to eat.
大多数动物宝宝都出生在春天，这时天气回暖，食物丰富。
When a chick is ready to hatch, it will use its special egg tooth, a sharp point on the end of its beak, to peck its way out of the egg.
当小鸡准备破壳时，它会用嘴尖上独特的“卵齿”（小尖突）啄破蛋壳钻出来。
It takes a whole day and night. No wonder chicks need a rest once they hatch!
这得花上一整天一整夜呢。难怪小鸡一孵出来就需要好好睡上一觉！
When chicks first hatch, they look damp and straggly, but they soon dry out and become fluffy.
刚孵出的小鸡羽毛湿漉漉、乱蓬蓬的，但很快就会晾干，变得毛茸茸、圆滚滚。
Ducks hatch in springtime too. The baby ducklings will follow their mum in a line to the pond to swim and find food together.
鸭宝宝也是在春天破壳的。小鸭子们会排成整齐的一队跟在妈妈身后走向池塘，一起游泳、找好吃的。
When lambs are first born, they are very wobbly on their legs. It takes a little while for them to stand up as their mother licks them clean.
小羊刚出生时，双腿晃晃悠悠站不稳。羊妈妈会温柔地把它们舔干净，过一会儿它们就能站起来啦。
Animal babies to spot: Chick: they might be yellow, brown, white or black. Lamb: look out for the littlest ones hidden in the grass. Calf: you might see one feeding on its mum's milk. Duckling: they have little pointy wings and big feet. Foal: you might see one sticking close to its mum.
寻找动物宝宝：小鸡：可能是黄色、棕色、白色或黑色。小羊：找找藏在草丛里最小的小羊。小牛：你可能会看到正在吃妈妈母乳的小牛。小鸭：它们长着尖尖的小翅膀和大脚掌。小马驹：你可能会看到紧紧依偎在妈妈身边的小马。
📍 Green Meadow Grass · 丰美的青草
Grass is growing, green and sweet, just right for a horse to eat.
青草长得又绿又甜，正好给小马当美餐。
Look what I found! A fat grass stalk I can put between my thumbs and blow. Squeak!
快看我找到了什么！一根粗粗的草茎，夹在大拇指中间用力吹——吱——！
Can you also see: One tractor, Two swooping birds, Three yellow flowers?
你还能找到这些吗：一辆拖拉机，两只俯冲飞翔的小鸟，三朵黄色的小花？
In springtime, the grass starts sprouting in the meadows. It is perfect food for the horses, cows and sheep.
到了春天，草地上的青草开始抽芽生长。对马、牛和羊来说，这是最美味的食物。
In late spring, the grass needs to be cut. The cuttings will be dried out in the sun and made into bales of hay for the animals to eat when winter comes again.
暮春时节需要收割牧草。割下的草晒干后会捆成干草垛，等冬天再次来临时给动物们吃。
The hay is stored in a hay barn to keep it dry. If the hay gets damp, it will grow mouldy and the animals won't be able to eat it.
干草存放在草料棚里保持干燥。如果干草受潮，就会发霉变质，动物们就不能吃啦。
Farm animals are usually kept in barns through the winter, but as soon as it's warm enough, they are let out into the grass meadows. They often get very excited when they first go outside, galloping around the field.
农场动物通常在棚舍里度过冬天，但只要天气暖和起来，它们就会被放到青草甸上。刚出门时它们总是格外兴奋，欢快地在田野上撒欢飞奔。
Types of wild grass to spot: Grasses with soft flower tufts. Feathery, fluffy grasses that wave in the breeze. Droopy grasses that bend over like mini arches. Long, spiky-looking grasses that stand up straight.
寻找野草种类：顶着柔软花穗的草。羽毛般蓬松、随风摇曳的草。像迷你拱门一样弯垂下头的草。笔直挺立、长着尖刺模样的草。
📍 Along the Hedgerow · 沿着绿树篱
Along the hedge there's lots to see, and a game for you and me.
沿着树篱看点真多，你和我来玩个小游戏。
Look what I found! Fluffy balls of dandelion seeds. Blow one and make a wish!
快看我找到了什么！毛茸茸的蒲公英种子球。吹一下，许个愿吧！
Can you also see: One mouse hiding away, Two soft spiders' webs, Three ladybirds on leaves?
你还能找到这些吗：一只藏起来的小老鼠，两张柔软的蜘蛛网，三只叶片上的小瓢虫？
Hedgerows are leafy homes for plants and animals. They give shelter from the wind and rain.
树篱是动植物枝繁叶茂的家园，能帮它们挡风遮雨。
Small birds and little animals make nests in a hedgerow's leafy shadows, away from bigger animals that might want to eat them.
小鸟和小动物在树篱的绿荫下筑巢，躲避可能吃掉它们的大型动物。
It's a safe home for minibeasts too.
这里也是微小昆虫们安全的庇护所。
A ball of dandelion seeds is sometimes called a clock because you can play a pretend game about telling the time.
蒲公英种子球有时被称为“蒲公英钟”，因为你可以玩一个假装报时的小游戏。
Count each time you blow the seeds until they are all gone. The first blow is one o'clock. What time will the last blow be?
每吹一次就数一下，直到种子全部吹飞。第一下是一点钟。最后一下吹散时会是几点钟呢？
The floaty dandelion seeds will blow away in the wind, and new dandelions will grow where the seeds land.
轻飘飘的蒲公英种子随风远去，落在哪里，哪里就会长出新的蒲公英。
Wildflowers often grow under the shelter of a hedge. It's okay to pick dandelion clocks and daisies because there are so many around, but it's best to leave other flowers alone.
野花常常在树篱的庇护下生长。采摘蒲公英绒球和雏菊是可以的，因为周围长着很多很多；但其他野花最好还是留在原地欣赏哦。
Types of minibeast to spot: Bumblebee: collecting nectar from the flowers. Butterfly: sucking up the sweet flower nectar. Spider: spinning its webs between the branches. Beetle: munching on plants or hunting other minibeasts. Aphid: feeding on sweet leaf juice called sap.
寻找微小生物：大黄蜂：在花丛中采集花蜜。蝴蝶：吸食甜甜的花蜜。蜘蛛：在枝丫间织网。甲虫：大嚼植物叶片或捕食其他小虫。蚜虫：吸食叶片里甜甜的汁液（树汁）。
📍 Trees and Birds · 树梢与飞鸟
Birds are perching in the trees. Their songs are floating on the breeze.
小鸟停歇在树梢上，歌声随微风四处飘扬。
Look what I found! A broken eggshell, blue as the sky and dotted with freckles.
快看我找到了什么！一枚破损的蛋壳，像天空一样蔚蓝，还落满了小雀斑。
Can you also see: One bird's nest, Two little rabbits, Three blackbirds?
你还能找到这些吗：一个鸟窝，两只小兔子，三只乌鸫（黑鸟）？
Wild birds build their nests in hedges and trees, and even in the barns on the farm.
野鸟在树篱和树上筑巢，有时甚至把窝安在农场的谷仓里。
When baby birds hatch, their broken eggs sometimes fall to the ground.
当鸟宝宝破壳而出时，破碎的蛋壳有时会掉落到地面上。
Some animals build their homes on the ground, or even underground.
有些动物在地面上做窝，有的甚至住在地底下。
Wild rabbits live in a burrow of underground tunnels called a warren. They pop up to nibble the meadow grass.
野兔住在由地下隧道组成的洞穴群（兔子窝，warren）里。它们会探出小脑袋啃食草地上的青草。
Bigger wild animals like foxes or badgers might live in quiet corners of a field.
像狐狸或獾这样较大的野生动物，可能住在田野僻静的角落。
A fox's underground home is called a den, and a badger's home is called a sett.
狐狸在地下的家叫巢穴（den），獾的家叫獾洞（sett）。
Animal homes to spot: Big entrance hole: foxes or badgers might live here. Medium-size entrance hole: rabbits might live here. Their droppings could be scattered around the entrances. Small entrance hole: rats or mice might live here. Nest: the bigger the bird, the bigger the nest they build.
寻找动物的家：大洞口：狐狸或獾可能住在这里。中等洞口：兔子可能住在这里，洞口周围可能散落着它们的便便。小洞口：田鼠或小老鼠可能住在这里。鸟窝：鸟儿越大，筑的巢也越大。
📍 Blossom and Crops · 花瓣与作物
Blossom petals drifting down, like confetti on the ground.
花瓣翩跹飘落地上，宛如散落一地的五彩纸屑。
Look what I found! An apple blossom petal, velvety between my fingers.
快看我找到了什么！一片苹果花瓣，摸在指尖像天鹅绒般细腻。
Can you also see: One watering can, Two bird feeders, Three woodpigeons?
你还能找到这些吗：一把喷壶，两个喂鸟器，三只斑尾林鸽？
Life on the farm changes all year round as different crops and fruits grow.
随着各种作物与水果的生长，农场的生活一年四季都在变化。
Some farms have a shop where you can buy fresh food that the farmer has grown.
有些农场开有小店，你可以在那里买到农夫亲手种出的新鲜食物。
The fruit trees blossom in spring and grow fruit in the summer.
果树在春天开花，在夏天结果。
Delicious apples, pears and cherries will one day be ready to eat in the sunshine.
美味的苹果、梨和樱桃，终有一天能在阳光下成熟供人品尝。
The bees that live on the farm make sweet, gooey honey through the spring and summer. In winter, they rest in their hives.
生活在农场的蜜蜂在春夏两季酿造香甜黏稠的蜂蜜。到了冬天，它们便在蜂巢里休息。
In summer, crops like wheat and barley grow taller and taller in the fields, ready to harvest before winter comes.
夏天里，田里的小麦和大麦越长越高，准备赶在冬天到来前收获。
Wheat will be turned into flour, perfect for bread and cakes.
小麦会被磨成面粉，最适合用来烤面包和做蛋糕。
Milk from cows, goats or sheep on the farm can be turned into other foods like butter, cheese, yoghurt or ice cream.
农场里奶牛、山羊或绵羊产出的奶，可以制成黄油、奶酪、酸奶或冰淇淋等各种美食。
Crops and fruit to spot: Wheat: the wheat grows in spiky tufts called ears. Oats: grains of oats are made into porridge and yummy flapjacks. Barley: grains of barley are used to make all sorts of things, including breakfast cereal. Fruit: look out for unusual fruit, such as mini crab apples and round, browny-orange medlars.
寻找作物与水果：小麦：麦粒长在带芒刺的花穗上，叫做麦穗。燕麦：燕麦粒被做成燕麦粥和美味的燕麦饼。大麦：大麦粒被用来制作各种食品，包括早餐麦片。水果：找找不同寻常的水果，例如小巧的山荆子（野苹果）和圆圆的棕橙色欧楂果。
📍 Farm Treasures & Explorer Code · 农场寻宝图与探索守则
We've seen the farm and walked around. Look at all the things we've found!
我们逛过了农场，漫步各处。快看看我们找到的所有宝物！
Treasure!
宝物清单！
As you collect, be thoughtful too. Bring no harm with what you do.
采集宝物时，多一份体贴细心。行动要轻柔，莫伤害自然生灵。
When you're exploring the farm, be careful around farm animals and wild animals so you don't scare them.
在农场探索时，靠近家畜和野生动物要格外小心，别吓到了它们。
Remember that it's their home, so be sure to look after it. And take your litter away with you.
请记住这里是它们的家，一定要用心呵护。走的时候记得把垃圾带走哦。
