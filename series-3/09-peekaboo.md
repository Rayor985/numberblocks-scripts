# 第 9 集 Ep 9：Peekaboo!｜躲猫猫

**剧情简介 Synopsis:** 躲猫猫啦！谁大谁小，一眼就看出来。/ Peekaboo! Who's hiding? Who's bigger, who's smaller — you can see at a glance!

**角色 Characters:** 一号 One、五号 Five、十号 Ten

**剧本 Script:**

*（大家玩躲猫猫 / Everyone plays hide and seek）*

**One（一号）:** Peekaboo! Where is Ten? / 躲猫猫！十号藏哪儿了？

*（十号躲在小树后，露出一大截 / Ten hides behind a small tree, sticking way out）*

**Five（五号）:** Haha! Ten is too big to hide! / 哈哈！十号太大，藏不住！

**Ten（十号）:** Ten is bigger than five! / 十比五大！

**One（一号）:** And One is smaller than five! Peekaboo! / 一比五小！躲猫猫！

*（一号躲进小盒子，完美藏好 / One hides inside a tiny box — a perfect fit）*

**Five（五号）:** Where is One? Peekaboo — found you! / 一号在哪儿？躲猫猫——找到啦！

**Five（五号）:** 🎵 Peekaboo, I see you, / 🎵 躲猫猫，我看到你，

🎵 Big or small, we all peek through! / 🎵 大或小，都现形！

🎵 One is small and Ten is tall, / 🎵 一号小，十号高，

🎵 Peekaboo — I found you all! / 🎵 躲猫猫——全找到！

**All（大家）:** Peekaboo! / 躲猫猫！
