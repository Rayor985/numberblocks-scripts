# 第 19 集 Ep 19：Mirror, Mirror｜魔镜魔镜

**剧情简介 Synopsis:** 神奇的魔镜告诉你：乘法就是"几个几"！三个二是多少？ / The magic mirror reveals: multiplication is just "how many lots of how many"! How much are three twos?

**角色 Characters:** 六号 Six、八号 Eight、魔镜 Magic Mirror

**剧本 Script:**

*（一面神奇的魔镜 / A magic mirror）*

**Six（六号）:** Mirror, mirror, what do you see? / 魔镜魔镜，你看到了什么？

**Magic Mirror（魔镜）:** I see… three twos! / 我看到……三个二！

*（镜子里出现 3 组 2 个方块 / Three groups of two blocks appear in the mirror）*

**Six（六号）:** Three twos are six! 3 × 2 = 6! / 三个二就是六！3 × 2 = 6！

**Eight（八号）:** My turn! Mirror, mirror! / 轮到我了！魔镜魔镜！

**Magic Mirror（魔镜）:** I see… two fours! / 我看到……两个四！

**Eight（八号）:** Two fours are eight! 2 × 4 = 8! / 两个四就是八！2 × 4 = 8！

**Six（六号）:** So multiplication is just "how many lots of how many"! / 原来乘法就是"几个几"！

**Magic Mirror（魔镜）:** Four threes are…? / 四个三是……？

**Eight（八号）:** Twelve! 4 × 3 = 12! / 十二！4 × 3 = 12！

**All（大家）:** Mirror, mirror, maths is magic! / 魔镜魔镜，数学真神奇！
