# 第 10 集 Ep 10：Hiccups｜打嗝

**剧情简介 Synopsis:** 哎哟打嗝了！每打一个嗝，6 到 10 就散开一次。/ Uh oh — hiccups! Every hiccup splits Six to Ten apart!

**角色 Characters:** 六号 Six、七号 Seven、八号 Eight、九号 Nine、十号 Ten

**剧本 Script:**

*（六号打了个嗝，散成两半 / Six gets the hiccups and splits in two）*

**Six（六号）:** Hic! Three and three make six! / 嗝！三加三等于六！

**Seven（七号）:** Hic! Oh no, the hiccups! / 嗝！糟糕，打嗝了！

*（七号散成三和四 / Seven splits into three and four）*

**Seven（七号）:** Hic! Three and four make seven! / 嗝！三加四等于七！

**Eight（八号）:** Hic! My turn! Five and three make eight! / 嗝！轮到我了！五加三等于八！

**Nine（九号）:** Hic! Four and five make nine! / 嗝！四加五等于九！

**Ten（十号）:** Hic! Six and four make ten! / 嗝！六加四等于十！

*（大家重新合体 / Everyone joins back together）*

**Seven（七号）:** Phew! Hiccups gone! / 呼！嗝打完了！

**All（大家）:** Six to ten — whole again! / 六到十——合体回来啦！
