# 第 4 集 Ep 4：Fruit Salad｜水果沙拉

**剧情简介 Synopsis:** 做水果沙拉啦！把水果分成两份，数字分合看得见。/ Let's make fruit salad! Split the fruit into two parts and watch numbers come apart!

**角色 Characters:** 五号 Five、三号 Three、四号 Four、六号 Six

**剧本 Script:**

*（厨房里，水果堆成山 / In the kitchen, piles of fruit）*

**Five（五号）:** Let's make fruit salad! Five strawberries! / 做水果沙拉啦！五个草莓！

*（草莓分成两份：2 个和 3 个 / The strawberries split into two parts: 2 and 3）*

**Five（五号）:** Two and three make five! / 二加三等于五！

**Three（三号）:** My turn! Four blueberries! / 轮到我了！四个蓝莓！

*（蓝莓分成 1 个和 3 个 / The blueberries split into 1 and 3）*

**Three（三号）:** One and three make four! / 一加三等于四！

**Six（六号）:** Six grapes! Three and three! / 六颗葡萄！三加三！

*（葡萄分成两份，每份三个 / The grapes split into two threes）*

**Six（六号）:** Three and three make six! / 三加三等于六！

**Four（四号）:** Mix them all together… / 把它们都拌在一起……

**All（大家）:** …yummy fruit salad! Numbers are delicious! / ……好吃的水果沙拉！数字真美味！
