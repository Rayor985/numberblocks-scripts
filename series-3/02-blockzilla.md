# 第 2 集 Ep 2：Blockzilla｜大怪兽

**剧情简介 Synopsis:** 大怪兽 Blockzilla 只吃更大的数字！谁大谁小，比一比就知道。/ Blockzilla only eats the BIGGER number! Who's bigger? Let's compare!

**角色 Characters:** 大怪兽 Blockzilla、三号 Three、五号 Five、四号 Four、七号 Seven、六号 Six

**剧本 Script:**

*（地面震动，大怪兽登场 / The ground shakes — Blockzilla stomps in）*

**Blockzilla（大怪兽）:** ROAR! I'm Blockzilla! I only eat the BIGGER number! / 吼！我是大怪兽！我只吃更大的数字！

**Three（三号）:** Eek! Three is… small! / 呀！三号……很小！

**Five（五号）:** Don't worry! Five is greater than three! / 别怕！五比三更大！

*（大怪兽的大嘴张向五号 / Blockzilla's big mouth opens towards Five）*

**Blockzilla（大怪兽）:** Five is greater than three! YUM! / 五大于三！好吃！

**Four（四号）:** Oh no! Now it's Seven's turn! / 糟糕！轮到七号了！

**Seven（七号）:** Seven is greater than four! / 七比四更大！

**Blockzilla（大怪兽）:** Seven is greater than four! YUM YUM! / 七大于四！真好吃！

**Six（六号）:** Wait! Six and six are the SAME! / 等等！六和六一样大！

*（两个六号站在一起，大怪兽困惑了 / Two Sixes stand together — Blockzilla is confused）*

**Blockzilla（大怪兽）:** Six equals six… nobody is bigger! / 六等于六……谁也不更大！

**Six（六号）:** That's right! And friends don't eat friends! / 没错！朋友是不吃朋友的！

**Blockzilla（大怪兽）:** …OK! Blockzilla will share instead! / ……好吧！大怪兽要学会分享！

**All（大家）:** Hooray for friendly Blockzilla! / 为友善的大怪兽欢呼！
