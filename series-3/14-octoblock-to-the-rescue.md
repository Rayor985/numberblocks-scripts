# 第 14 集 Ep 14：Octoblock to the Rescue!｜Octoblock 来救援！

**剧情简介 Synopsis:** Octoblock 来救援！8 有好多种分法，每种分法都能帮忙。/ Octoblock to the rescue! So many ways to make eight — and every way can help!

**角色 Characters:** 八号 Eight、一号 One、七号 Seven、四号 Four、三号 Three、五号 Five

**剧本 Script:**

*（警报响起，有人需要帮助 / Alarm! Someone needs help）*

**Eight（八号）:** Octoblock to the rescue! / Octoblock 来救援！

*（一号被困在高高的树枝上 / One is stuck on a high branch）*

**One（一号）:** Help! I'm stuck! / 救命！我下不来了！

**Eight（八号）:** Split into seven and one! / 分成七和一！

**Seven（七号）:** Seven and one make eight! I'll lift you down! / 七加一等于八！我来抱你下来！

*（小桥断了，两边要一样重才平衡 / A little bridge is broken — both sides must balance）*

**Eight（八号）:** Split into four and four! / 分成四和四！

**Four（四号）:** Four and four make eight! Perfect balance! / 四加四等于八！完美平衡！

*（要搬八块石头 / Eight stones need carrying）*

**Eight（八号）:** Split into three and five! / 分成三和五！

**Three（三号）:** Three stones for me… / 三块石头归我……

**Five（五号）:** …and five stones for me! Three and five make eight! / ……五块石头归我！三加五等于八！

*（八号合体 / Eight joins back together）*

**Eight（八号）:** One and seven, four and four, three and five — so many ways to make eight! / 一加七、四加四、三加五——组成八的方法真多！

**All（大家）:** Hooray for Octoblock! / 为 Octoblock 欢呼！
