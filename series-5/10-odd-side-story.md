# 第 10 集 Ep 10：Odd Side Story｜奇数外传

> 注：原创台词（非官方实录）/ Original dialogue, not an official transcript.

**剧情简介 Synopsis:** 奇数加奇数得偶数？正方形数竟然是奇数一个个加起来的！/ Odd plus odd makes even? And square numbers are made by adding odd numbers one by one!

**角色 Characters:** 一号 One、三号 Three、五号 Five、九号 Nine、十六 Sixteen、八号 Eight

**剧本 Script:**

*（奇数队和偶数队登场 / The odd team and even team arrive）*

**One（一号）:** I'm odd! One, three, five — we odds always have one left over! / 我是奇数！一、三、五——我们奇数总会多出一个！

**Eight（八号）:** And I'm even! Two, four, six, eight — we split into pairs! / 我是偶数！二、四、六、八——我们都能成双成对！

**Three（三号）:** Odd and odd make even! Three and five make eight! / 奇加奇得偶！三加五得八！

**Eight（八号）:** Even and even make even! Two and six make eight! / 偶加偶得偶！二加六得八！

**Five（五号）:** Odd and even make odd! Three and four make seven! / 奇加偶得奇！三加四得七！

**Nine（九号）:** Look at me! One and three and five make nine — I'm a square made of odds! / 看我！一加三加五得九——我是奇数拼成的正方形！

**Sixteen（十六）:** One and three and five and seven make sixteen! Me too! / 一加三加五加七得十六！我也是！

**One（一号）:** One, four, nine, sixteen… squares love odd numbers! / 一、四、九、十六……正方形数爱奇数！

**All（大家）:** Odd side story — what a discovery! / 奇数外传——大发现！
