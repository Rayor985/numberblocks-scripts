# 第 7 集 Ep 7：Ten Vaulting｜撑杆跳过十

> 注：原创台词（非官方实录）/ Original dialogue, not an official transcript.

**剧情简介 Synopsis:** 七加八太大数不过来？别怕！先凑成十，再跳过去！/ Seven plus eight is too big to count? No fear! Make ten first, then vault over!

**角色 Characters:** 七号 Seven、八号 Eight、十号 Ten、五号 Five

**剧本 Script:**

*（运动场，十号躺在地上当"横杆" / Sports field, Ten lies down as the "bar"）*

**Seven（七号）:** Seven and eight… that's too big to count on my fingers! / 七加八……手指头数不过来了！

**Ten（十号）:** Vault over me! Split eight into three and five! / 从我身上跳过去！把八分成三和五！

**Seven（七号）:** Seven and three make ten… / 七加三得十……

*（七号撑杆跳过十号 / Seven pole-vaults over Ten）*

**Seven（七号）:** …then five more! Ten and five make fifteen! / ……再加五！十加五得十五！

**Eight（八号）:** Seven plus eight is fifteen! / 七加八等于十五！

**Five（五号）:** My turn! Eight and five… eight and two make ten, then three more! / 轮到我了！八加五……八加二得十，再加三！

**Eight（八号）:** Ten and three make thirteen! / 十加三得十三！

**Ten（十号）:** Make ten first, then add the rest! / 先凑成十，再加剩下的！

**All（大家）:** Ten vaulting! Over we go! / 撑杆跳过十！跳过去啦！
