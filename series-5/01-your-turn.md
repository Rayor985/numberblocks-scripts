# 第 1 集 Ep 1：Your Turn｜轮到你了

> 注：原创台词（非官方实录）/ Original dialogue, not an official transcript.

**剧情简介 Synopsis:** 大家排排站上数轴，比一比谁大谁小——每个人都有轮到的机会！/ Everyone lines up on the number line to compare who's bigger — and everyone gets a turn!

**角色 Characters:** 一号 One、二号 Two、三号 Three、四号 Four、五号 Five、六号 Six

**剧本 Script:**

*（数字乐园，一条长长的数轴 / Numberland, a long number line）*

**One（一号）:** It's my turn! I'm One, the smallest! / 轮到我了！我是一号，最小的！

**Two（二号）:** My turn! Two is bigger than One! / 轮到我了！二比一更大！

**Three（三号）:** Three is bigger than Two! Step onto the number line! / 三比二更大！站上数轴来！

*（大家依次站上数轴 1 到 6 / Everyone steps onto the number line, 1 to 6）*

**Four（四号）:** Look! The further along, the bigger the number! / 看！越往后，数字越大！

**Five（五号）:** Five is bigger than Four! Am I the biggest? / 五比四更大！我是最大的吗？

**Six（六号）:** Not quite! Six is bigger than Five! / 还差一点！六比五更大！

**One（一号）:** Six is the biggest… but One is the smallest, and that's special too! / 六是最大的……但一是最小的，这也很特别！

**Two（二号）:** Who's bigger than Three? Four and Five and Six! / 谁比三更大？四、五、六！

**All（大家）:** Your turn, my turn — everyone gets a turn! / 你来我来——人人都有机会！
