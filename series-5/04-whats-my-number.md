# 第 4 集 Ep 4：What's My Number?｜猜猜我是几？

> 注：原创台词（非官方实录）/ Original dialogue, not an official transcript.

**剧情简介 Synopsis:** 不用数，一眼看出是几个！Numberblocks 们玩起"一眼认数"游戏。/ No counting needed — see how many at a glance! The Numberblocks play the "see it in a flash" game.

**角色 Characters:** 一号 One、三号 Three、五号 Five、七号 Seven、十号 Ten

**剧本 Script:**

*（游戏时间，一块大幕布 / Game time, a big curtain）*

**One（一号）:** Look at me! How many blocks? One! No counting needed! / 看我！几个方块？一个！不用数！

**Three（三号）:** Three! I can see it in a flash! / 三个！我一眼就看出来了！

*（幕布快速掀开又合上，露出五个方块 / The curtain flashes open and shut, showing five blocks）*

**Five（五号）:** Five! Five blocks, just like that! / 五个！一眼就看出来了！

**Seven（七号）:** Hmm, seven is tricky… count the rows: three and four! / 嗯，七有点难……数数行：三加四！

**Ten（十号）:** Ten! Two rows of five — easy! / 十个！两排五——简单！

**One（一号）:** What's my number? Guess in a flash! / 猜猜我是几？一眼看出来！

**Three（三号）:** See the pattern, know the number! / 看出图案，就知道数字！

**All（大家）:** Subitising! See it in a flash! / 一眼认数！看一眼就知道！
