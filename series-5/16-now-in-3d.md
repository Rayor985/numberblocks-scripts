# 第 16 集 Ep 16：Now in 3D｜立体世界

**剧情简介 Synopsis:** 二号和三号把自己折成立方体：2×2×2=8，3×3×3=27，数字乐园进入立体世界！/ Two and Three fold themselves into cubes — 2×2×2=8 and 3×3×3=27 — and Numberland goes 3D!

**角色 Characters:** 二号 Two、三号 Three、八号 Eight、二十七 Twenty-seven

**剧本 Script:**

*（场景：平面世界，一切都是平的 / Scene: flatland, everything is flat）*

**Two（二号）:** I'm flat! Two by one! / 我是平的！2×1！

**Three（三号）:** Me too! Three by one! / 我也是！3×1！

**Two（二号）:** What if we folded… upwards? / 如果我们向上折起来呢？

*（二号把自己折成 2×2×2 的立方体 / Two folds into a 2×2×2 cube）*

**Eight（八号）:** I'm Eight! A cube! Two layers of two by two! / 我是八号！一个立方体！两层 2×2！

**Three（三号）:** My turn! Fold, fold, fold! / 轮到我了！折、折、折！

*（三号把自己折成 3×3×3 的立方体 / Three folds into a 3×3×3 cube）*

**Twenty-seven（二十七）:** I'm Twenty-seven! A bigger cube! Three layers of three by three! / 我是二十七！更大的立方体！三层 3×3！

**Eight（八号）:** 2 by 2 by 2 is 8! / 2×2×2 等于 8！

**Twenty-seven（二十七）:** 3 by 3 by 3 is 27! / 3×3×3 等于 27！

**All（大家）:** We're in 3D now! / 我们进入立体世界啦！
