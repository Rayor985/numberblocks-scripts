# 第 13 集 Ep 13：The Team Factor｜组队因数

> 注：原创台词（非官方实录）/ Original dialogue, not an official transcript.

**剧情简介 Synopsis:** 因数就是组队密码！能分成相等小队的就是合数，分不了的就是质数！/ Factors are the secret to teaming up! Numbers that split into equal teams are composite; those that can't are prime!

**角色 Characters:** 十二 Twelve、十五 Fifteen、十三 Thirteen

**剧本 Script:**

*（操场上，大家玩组队游戏 / On the playground, everyone plays the team game）*

**Twelve（十二）:** Split into teams! Two teams of six! / 分组！两队，每队六人！

**Twelve（十二）:** Or three teams of four! Or four teams of three! / 或者三队，每队四人！或者四队，每队三人！

**Fifteen（十五）:** Three teams of five! Five teams of three! / 三队，每队五人！五队，每队三人！

**Thirteen（十三）:** My turn… one team of thirteen? That's no fun! / 轮到我了……一队十三人？不好玩！

**Thirteen（十三）:** Try two teams… one left over! Three teams… one left over! / 试两队……多一个！三队……还多一个！

**Twelve（十二）:** You can't split into equal teams — you're prime! / 你分不成相等的小队——你是质数！

**Thirteen（十三）:** Prime and proud! One and thirteen are my only factors! / 质数也骄傲！一和十三是我仅有的因数！

**All（大家）:** Factors are team-makers! Primes stand alone! / 因数来组队！质数独自站！
