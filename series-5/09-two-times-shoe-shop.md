# 第 9 集 Ep 9：Two Times Shoe Shop ♫｜二乘法表鞋店

> 注：原创台词（非官方实录）/ Original dialogue, not an official transcript.

**剧情简介 Synopsis:** 二乘法表开了一家鞋店！鞋子都成双卖——一乘二得二，二乘二得四……/ The Two Times Table opens a shoe shop! Shoes are sold in pairs — one times two is two, two times two is four…

**角色 Characters:** 二乘法表 Two Times Table、二号 Two

**剧本 Script:**

*（鞋店开张，橱窗里摆满成双的鞋 / The shoe shop opens, windows full of pairs of shoes）*

**Two Times Table（二乘法表）:** Welcome to the Two Times Shoe Shop! Shoes come in pairs! / 欢迎来到二乘法表鞋店！鞋子都成双卖！

**Two（二号）:** One pair is two shoes! One times two is two! / 一双就是两只鞋！一乘二得二！

**Two Times Table（二乘法表）:** Two pairs is four shoes! Two times two is four! / 两双就是四只鞋！二乘二得四！

**Two（二号）:** Three pairs… two times three is six! / 三双……二乘三得六！

**Two Times Table（二乘法表）:** All the way to ten pairs — two times ten is twenty! / 一直到十双——二乘十得二十！

🎵 Two shoes make a pair, pairs are fun to wear! / 两只鞋成一双，穿上真漂亮！

🎵 Two times two is four, two times three is six! / 二乘二得四，二乘三得六！

🎵 Two times ten is twenty — count in twos, that's the trick! / 二乘十得二十——数双数，就是这么灵！

**Two（二号）:** Pairs for everyone! / 人人都有新鞋穿！
