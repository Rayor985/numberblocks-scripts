# 第 10 集 Ep 10：Octoblock and the Path of Justice｜Octoblock 与正义之路

**剧情简介 Synopsis:** 捣蛋鬼 Octonaughty 在市中心捣乱！Octoblock 看着地图追踪他——能及时赶到吗？/ Octonaughty is up to no good in the town centre! Octoblock sets out to track him down using a map — but will he reach him in time?

**角色 Characters:** 八号 Eight（Octoblock）、捣蛋鬼 Octonaughty

**剧本 Script:**

*（市中心，捣蛋鬼在捣乱 / Town centre, Octonaughty causing trouble）*

**Octonaughty（捣蛋鬼）:** Hehehe! I'm Octonaughty, and I'm up to no good! / 嘿嘿嘿！我是捣蛋鬼，我要捣乱！

**Eight（八号）:** Not so fast! Octoblock is on the path of justice! / 别跑！Octoblock 踏上正义之路！

**Eight（八号）:** The map says: past the square, over the bridge… / 地图上说：穿过广场，过小桥……

**Octonaughty（捣蛋鬼）:** You'll never catch me! / 你永远抓不到我！

**Eight（八号）:** Through the tunnel, round the corner… got you! / 穿过隧道，绕过街角……抓到你了！

**Octonaughty（捣蛋鬼）:** Oh no! Foiled again! / 糟糕！又失败了！

**Eight（八号）:** Eight blocks, one hero — justice wins! / 八个方块，一位英雄——正义胜利！

**All（大家）:** Hooray for Octoblock! / 为 Octoblock 欢呼！
