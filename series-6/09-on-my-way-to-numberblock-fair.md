# 第 9 集 Ep 9：On My Way to Numberblock Fair｜去数字集市的路上

**剧情简介 Synopsis:** 一号拿着地图去数字集市：上、下、左、右，过桥、穿树，跟着方向走！/ One is on her way to Numberblock Fair, using positional language and a map: up, down, left, right!

**角色 Characters:** 一号 One、二号 Two

**剧本 Script:**

*（十字路口，一号拿着地图 / Crossroads, One holds a map）*

**One（一号）:** I'm on my way to Numberblock Fair! / 我要去数字集市啦！

**Two（二号）:** Follow the map! First: go straight ahead! / 跟着地图走！先：直走！

**One（一号）:** 🎵 Up and down and left and right, / 🎵 上、下、左、右，

**One（一号）:** 🎵 Follow the map, you'll be all right! / 🎵 跟着地图走，不会错！

**Two（二号）:** Over the bridge, under the tree… / 过小桥，穿过树下……

**One（一号）:** Over and under — I'm getting closer! / 上面下面——越来越近啦！

**Two（二号）:** Turn left at the big red door! / 在大红门左转！

**One（一号）:** I can see the fair! We made it! / 我看到集市啦！我们到啦！

**All（大家）:** The Numberblock Fair — hooray! / 数字集市——万岁！
