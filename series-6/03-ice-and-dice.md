# 第 3 集 Ep 3：Ice and Dice｜冰上骰子

**剧情简介 Synopsis:** 一号、二号、三号用骰子制造机做骰子，探索公平的秘密：每一面机会均等！/ One, Two and Three use a dice-making machine to discover fairness: every side has the same chance!

**角色 Characters:** 一号 One、二号 Two、三号 Three

**剧本 Script:**

*（溜冰场，骰子制造机 / Ice rink, a dice-making machine）*

**One（一号）:** A dice-making machine! Let's make a dice! / 骰子制造机！我们来做个骰子！

**Two（二号）:** A dice has… one, two, three, four, five, six sides! / 骰子有……一、二、三、四、五、六面！

**Three（三号）:** But is it fair? Let's roll it on the ice! / 可它公平吗？在冰上掷掷看！

*（掷了很多次，数结果 / They roll many times and count the results）*

**One（一号）:** Three came up twice! Six came up twice! / 三出现了两次！六出现了两次！

**Two（二号）:** Every side gets a turn — that's fair! / 每一面都有机会——这就是公平！

**Three（三号）:** One dice, six sides, six equal chances! / 一个骰子，六面，六种均等的机会！

**One（一号）:** No favourites — everyone gets a fair go! / 不偏心——人人机会均等！

**All（大家）:** Fair dice for everyone! / 公平的骰子，人人有份！
