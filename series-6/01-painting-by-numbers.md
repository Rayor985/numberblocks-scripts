# 第 1 集 Ep 1：Painting by Numbers｜按数字涂色

**剧情简介 Synopsis:** 三号给二号画像时多画了一个方块，还假扮二号蒙混过关——最后明白：重要的不是颜色，而是数字。/ Three paints Two with one too many blocks and disguises herself as Two — but learns it's the number that counts, not the colour.

**角色 Characters:** 三号 Three、二号 Two、一号 One

**剧本 Script:**

*（画室，画架和颜料 / Art studio, easels and paints）*

**Three（三号）:** I'm painting portraits! Smile, Two! / 我在画肖像画！二号，笑一个！

**Two（二号）:** One, two… say cheese! / 一、二……笑一个！

*（三号数着画，画了三个方块 / Three counts as she paints — three blocks）*

**Three（三号）:** Done! It's… Two! / 画好啦！这是……二号！

**One（一号）:** Hmm… that painting has three blocks. That's not Two! / 嗯……这幅画有三个方块。这不是二号！

**Three（三号）:** Oops! One too many! / 糟糕！多画了一个！

*（三号把自己涂成橙色 / Three paints herself orange）*

**Three（三号）:** Look! I'm Two now! / 看！我现在是二号了！

**One（一号）:** No, you're not! You're still Three — one, two, three blocks! / 才不是！你还是三号——一、二、三个方块！

**Three（三号）:** But I'm orange now! / 可我现在是橙色的了！

**One（一号）:** Colour doesn't count — the number counts! / 颜色不算数——数字才算数！

**Three（三号）:** You're right! I'm Three, three blocks, whatever colour! / 你说得对！我是三号，三个方块，不管什么颜色！

**All（大家）:** It's the number that counts! / 数字才是最重要的！
