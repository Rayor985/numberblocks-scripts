Series 6 · EP 1

Painting by Numbers

按数字涂色
📖 剧情简介

三号给二号画像时多画了一个方块，还假扮二号蒙混过关——最后明白：重要的不是颜色，而是数字。

Three paints Two with one too many blocks and disguises herself as Two — but learns it's the number that counts, not the colour.
