Shape Party

图形派对
📖 剧情简介

四号和二号潜入平面国参加图形派对：圆形、三角形、四边形，了解它们的边和角，最后来一场图形舞！

Four and Two dive into Flatland for a shape party! They meet circles, different-sized triangles and quadrilaterals, learn all about their sides and corners — then enjoy a shape dance.
