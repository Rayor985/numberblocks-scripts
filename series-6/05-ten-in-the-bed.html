Ten in the Bed

📖 剧情简介

十号唱起改编版摇篮曲《床上有十个》，哄小 Blobs 们在十格阵大床上一个个入睡。

Ten puts the Numberblobs to sleep by singing a special version of the nursery rhyme "Ten in the Bed", counting down in a ten-frame bed.
