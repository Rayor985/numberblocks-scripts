# 第 5 集 Ep 5：Ten in the Bed｜十个小床

**剧情简介 Synopsis:** 十号唱起改编版摇篮曲《床上有十个》，哄小 Blobs 们在十格阵大床上一个个入睡。/ Ten puts the Numberblobs to sleep by singing a special version of the nursery rhyme "Ten in the Bed", counting down in a ten-frame bed.

**角色 Characters:** 十号 Ten、小 Blobs Numberblobs

**剧本 Script:**

*（卧室，一张十格阵大床 / Bedroom, a big ten-frame bed）*

**Ten（十号）:** Time for bed, little Blobs! Snuggle into the ten-frame! / 小 Blobs 们，该睡觉了！钻进十格阵被窝！

**Ten（十号）:** 🎵 Ten in the bed, and the little blob said: / 🎵 床上有十个，小 Blob 说：

**Ten（十号）:** 🎵 "Roll over! Roll over!" / 🎵 "翻个身！翻个身！"

*（大家翻身，一个掉下去 / They all roll over, and one falls out）*

**Numberblobs（小 Blobs）:** Nine! / 九个！

**Ten（十号）:** 🎵 Nine in the bed… eight in the bed… / 🎵 床上有九个……床上有八个……

*（一个个掉下去：7，6，5，4，3，2 / One by one they tumble out: 7, 6, 5, 4, 3, 2）*

**Ten（十号）:** 🎵 One in the bed, and the little blob said: / 🎵 床上剩一个，小 Blob 说：

**Ten（十号）:** 🎵 "Good night, everyone!" / 🎵 "大家晚安！"

**All（大家）:** Shhh… the Blobs are asleep. / 嘘……小 Blobs 睡着啦。

**Ten（十号）:** Ten, nine, eight… all the way to one. Sweet dreams! / 十、九、八……一直数到一。做个好梦！
