# 第 8 集 Ep 8：Cuboid Castle｜长方体城堡

**剧情简介 Synopsis:** 给小 Blobs 建一座超棒的游乐城堡：正方体、长方体、棱锥、棱柱齐上阵！/ The Numberblocks build a magnificent play fort for the Numberblobs, exploring cubes, cuboids, pyramids and prisms.

**角色 Characters:** 八号 Eight、四号 Four、十号 Ten

**剧本 Script:**

*（建筑工地，各种立体形状 / Building site, 3D shapes everywhere）*

**Eight（八号）:** Let's build a castle for the Numberblobs! / 给小 Blobs 建座城堡吧！

**Four（四号）:** Cubes for the walls — every face is a square! / 用正方体砌墙——每个面都是正方形！

**Ten（十号）:** Cuboids for the towers — long and strong! / 用长方体建塔楼——又长又结实！

**Eight（八号）:** Pyramids for the roofs — pointy on top! / 用棱锥做屋顶——尖尖的！

**Four（四号）:** And prisms for the windows! / 棱柱做窗户！

**Ten（十号）:** A cuboid is like a stretched cube! / 长方体就像拉长的正方体！

**Eight（八号）:** Cube, cuboid, pyramid, prism — cuboid castle complete! / 正方体、长方体、棱锥、棱柱——长方体城堡完工！

**Numberblobs（小 Blobs）:** Hooray! Our castle! / 万岁！我们的城堡！
