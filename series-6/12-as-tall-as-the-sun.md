# 第 12 集 Ep 12：As Tall as the Sun｜和太阳一样高

**剧情简介 Synopsis:** 谁最高？Numberblocks 们叠罗汉比高、学测量，连太阳都来凑热闹！/ Who's the tallest? The Numberblocks stack up, measure and compare — and the sun joins in!

**角色 Characters:** 十号 Ten、五号 Five、太阳 Sun

**剧本 Script:**

*（大家比高 / Everyone compares heights）*

**Five（五号）:** I'm five blocks tall! / 我有五个方块高！

**Ten（十号）:** I'm ten blocks tall — taller than you! / 我有十个方块高——比你高！

**Five（五号）:** How do we know who's tallest? / 怎么知道谁最高？

**Ten（十号）:** Stand back to back and compare! / 背靠背比一比！

*（太阳升起来 / The sun rises）*

**Sun（太阳）:** I'm the sun — I'm taller than everyone! / 我是太阳——我比谁都高！

**Ten（十号）:** Nobody is as tall as the sun! / 没人有太阳高！

**Five（五号）:** Measuring is comparing! / 测量就是比较！

**All（大家）:** As tall as the sun? Not quite — but we tried! / 和太阳一样高？还差一点——但我们努力啦！
